## Long Life Prayer of the 14th Gyalwang ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/39. རྒྱལ་དབང་བཅུ་བཞི་པའི་ཞབས་བརྟན.md#^1-1]]

OM SVA STI — may all be auspicious. ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/39. རྒྱལ་དབང་བཅུ་བཞི་པའི་ཞབས་བརྟན.md#^1-2]]

You are the noble Avalokiteśvara himself, the supreme one who guides beings.
Protector of the Land of Snows, Tenzin Gyatso may your life remain firm and lasting.
In accordance with the intention of your stainless mind,
may you soon be enthroned in the Potala Palace,
and may the Buddha’s teachings flourish and spread. ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/39. རྒྱལ་དབང་བཅུ་བཞི་པའི་ཞབས་བརྟན.md#^1-3]]

and may the Buddha’s teachings flourish and spread. In the land surrounded by a ring of snow mountains,
you are the source of every benefit and happiness.
Avalokiteśvara, Tenzin Gyatso,
may your lotus feet remain steadfast until the end of existence. ^1-3
