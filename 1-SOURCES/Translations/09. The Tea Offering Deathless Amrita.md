# The Tea Offering: Deathless Amrita ^0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/09. ཇ་མཆོད་འཆི་མེད་བདུད་རྩི.md#^1-0]]

## The Tea Offering: Deathless Amrita ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/09. ཇ་མཆོད་འཆི་མེད་བདུད་རྩི.md#^1-1]]

Protector of samsara and nirvana, peerless embodiment of all refuges,
precious treasury of supreme and ordinary siddhis,
to my compassionate father root guru,
I offer this tea of deathless amrita! ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/09. ཇ་མཆོད་འཆི་མེད་བདུད་རྩི.md#^1-2]]

Protector of beings, glorious Khyentse Wangchuk,
Dharma Lord, Venerable Taklung Tangpa,
to you with your precious lineage,
I offer this tea of deathless amrita! ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/09. ཇ་མཆོད་འཆི་མེད་བདུད་རྩི.md#^1-3]]

In the countless mandalas of deities of the tantras,
Lord of the Sages, taming all who are to be tamed in every realm,
to all your heirs and all their disciples,
I offer this tea of deathless amrita! ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/09. ཇ་མཆོད་འཆི་མེད་བདུད་རྩི.md#^1-4]]

Long ago, you were appointed to protect the Dharma
Glorious Awareness Protector, together with your consort,
With the divine UpasakaYigyal (Lynx King) and his retinue,
I offer this tea of deathless amrita! ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/09. ཇ་མཆོད་འཆི་མེད་བདུད་རྩི.md#^1-5]]

In this life and in all of my future lives,
peerless holy protectors, from all of you,
may I never be separated even for an instant,
and may I realize the supreme Dharma of Mahamudra! ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/09. ཇ་མཆོད་འཆི་མེད་བདུད་རྩི.md#^1-6]]

Venerable Supreme Guide, Dharma King,
Your unchanging vajra body on a lion throne,
Your speech, the song of Brahma, like a rainfall of Dharma,
I pray you will protect your disciples with loving heart, ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/09. ཇ་མཆོད་འཆི་མེད་བདུད་རྩི.md#^1-7]]

establish our mother sentient beings in bliss, and
cause the doctrine of the Royal Sage to flourish, and
pacifying all opposing circumstances and obstacles,
may we quickly attain the supreme kingdom of Buddha! ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/09. ཇ་མཆོད་འཆི་མེད་བདུད་རྩི.md#^1-8]]

<small>So, after being requested by one steward who needed a tea offering prayer, I, Ngawang Namgyal Trashi, praised thus.</small> ^1-8
