## Prayer for the Flourishing of the Teachings of the Glorious Great Taklungpa Practice Lineage ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-1]]

Within this auspicious expanse of all that can be known, in saṃsāra and nirvāṇa,
Whatever glory and excellent qualities there are are gathered at your two lotus feet.
Taklungpa, by relying upon whose feet all attainments are easily bestowed,
To you, the lord of the Victorious Ones, immovable and unshaken, I bow my head in homage. ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-2]]

Known as GyéDzé Dorje, beautifully adorned with the ornament of ethical discipline,
Whose meaningful renown is the glory that dispels the darkness within,
Whose rays of realization spread throughout the vast fortunate age,
To Sangye Ön, who brings relief and liberation, I offer this prayer. ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-3]]

Victorious Ones dwelling in realms throughout all- pervading space, together with the Dharma they taught,
Bodhisattvas of the ten bhūmis, Śrāvakas, Pratyekabuddhas, and the teachings of discipline,
The Three Roots and all other authentic sources of refuge—
All of you who are valid and true, please come as witnesses to this prayer of aspiration. ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-4]]

From all the scriptural teachings of the Sage, beyond mere words,
The Dakpo lineage reveals primordial wisdom directly and vividly.
May the teachings of the glorious Taklungpa, who perfected devotion,
Flourish greatly, and may the communities of the Sangha increase! ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-5]]

The vows that restrain the faults of conditioned existence are the gateway to complete liberation.
Through the three trainings of ethical discipline, meditative concentration, and wisdom,
May the teachings of the glorious Taklungpa, whose pure conduct is stainless,
Flourish greatly, and may the communities of the Sangha increase! ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-6]]

The seed that causes peace and happiness, eliminates misfortune, and leads to omniscience,
Is engaging for others’ welfare in the ocean-like practice of the six pāramitās.
May the teachings of the glorious Taklungpa, supreme among the children of the Victorious Ones,
Flourish greatly, and may the communities of the Sangha increase! ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-7]]

The outer world and its inhabitants—all appearances, sounds, and thoughts—are primordial wisdom;
Within the mandala, they spontaneously arise as the deities in their various forms.
May the teachings of the glorious Taklungpa, supreme among Vidyādharas,
Flourish greatly, and may the communities of the Sangha increase! ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-8]]

Beautifully clothed in the three trainings and the conduct of awakening,
The supreme treasury of the four jewels of profound meaning,
May the teachings of the glorious Taklungpa, whose life of liberation is incomparable,
Flourish greatly, and may the communities of the Sangha increase! ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-9]]

May those who carefully restrain their conduct through discipline uphold their own Dharma tradition.
May they become greatly learned and properly establish a gentle and disciplined mind.
May the Sangha wearing the pure saffron-colored robes flourish,
Increasing beyond measure and becoming an ornament beautifying the teachings! ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-10]]

In the three worlds, it is the source of every benefit and happiness without exception,
And the medicine that pacifies suffering together with its causes.
May the Buddha’s teachings in general and the Taklungpa practice lineage in particular
Blaze brilliantly at the very summit of existence! ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-11]]

Through the compassionate blessings of the vast ocean of all the Three Jewels without exception,
Through the power of the words of those who speak the truth,
And through the swift and powerful activity of the vajra-bound oath-keeping protectors,
May every aspiration made here be completely accomplished! ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/48. སྒྲུབ་བརྒྱུད་དཔལ་ལྡན་སྟག་ལུང་པ་ཆེན་པོའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-12]]

<small>This was written by Taklungpa Chöying Lhündrub. May it be virtuous!</small> ^1-12
