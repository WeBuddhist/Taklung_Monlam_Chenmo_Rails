# Long-Life Prayer Called “May All Virtue and Goodness Increase” ^0

## Long-Life Prayer Called “May All Virtue and Goodness Increase” ^1-0

Lord of all, Vajradhara, who possesses the sevenfold union;
Tilopa, Nāropa, and the three—Marpa, Milarepa, and Dagpo;
root and lineage lamas, chosen deities, and the Three Jewels: may you bless this prayer.
May the ocean of refuge-objects grant auspicious things ^1-1

With an unwavering resolve to uphold the Victorious One’s teachings,
you raise the banner of the Dharma of universal goodness in every land.
You have the courage and sincere intention to do so without fear.
According to the needs and inclinations of the many beings to be guided, ^1-2

you bestow the fine nectar of the sutras, transmitted teachings, and essential instructions.
You have attained the excellence of learning and realization.
As a true support for the teachings and for beings, may you live long. ^1-3

To uphold the banner of the Taklung tradition— whose devotion has reached perfection,
and whose teachings are preserved through study and practice—
may you naturally possess every noble quality.
May you live long, while your activities arise spontaneously and extend everywhere at all times. ^1-4

By the profound power of the blessings of the Buddhas and their heirs,
and by the unwavering strength of the connection created through the devotion of myself and others,
may all that we pray for be fulfilled just as we wish.
May the light of virtue and goodness increase everywhere. ^1-5

Sarva maṅgalam may all be auspicious. How virtuous! ^1-6

<small>This prayer, entitled May All Virtue and Goodness Increase, asks that TaklungMatrul Tenzin Kunzang Jigme live long for the benefit of the teachings and beings, and that his activities flourish. The Taklung lamas and monks remaining in Tibet requested it through Gyaltsen Lungtok, whom they sent to serve the tulku and who presented the request with a ceremonial scarf. In response, the Buddhist monk Tenzin Gyatso composed it on August 2, 1989, corresponding to the first day of the sixth month of the Tibetan Earth Snake year.</small> ^1-7
