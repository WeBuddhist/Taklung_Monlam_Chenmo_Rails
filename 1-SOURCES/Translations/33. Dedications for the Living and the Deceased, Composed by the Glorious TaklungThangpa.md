# Dedications for the Living and the Deceased, Composed by the Glorious TaklungThangpa ^0

## Dedications for the Living and the Deceased, Composed by the Glorious TaklungThangpa ^33-0

### Dedication for the Living ^33-1-0

All Buddhas and Bodhisattvas dwelling throughout the ten directions, please turn your attention toward us. ^33-1

Through the roots of virtue that we have accomplished in this way, may the precious teachings of the Buddha— the source of benefit and happiness in general—flourish and spread throughout all directions, times, places, and circumstances, together with abundant offerings and reverence, and may they remain for a very long time. ^33-2

May all the extraordinary holy beings who uphold the teachings enjoy good health and long lives, and may their completely pure enlightened activities spread throughout the ten directions. ^33-3

May the extraordinary enlightened intentions of all the glorious and holy former gurus who have passed peacefully away be fulfilled to ever higher and higher degrees. ^33-4

May all obstacles to the lives, deeds, and enlightened activities of the glorious and holy gurus who presently remain among us be completely pacified. ^33-5

Through their blessings and spiritual power, may all adverse circumstances, obstacles, and unfavorable conditions affecting the person named [name] be completely pacified. ^33-6

May all favorable conditions and all desired aims that accord with the Dharma be perfectly and effortlessly accomplished according to their wishes. ^33-7

May life, merit, glory, prosperity, and every kind of good fortune increase and expand more and more, like the waxing moon. ^33-8

May they be completely free from every form of decline and misfortune, both worldly and transcendent. ^33-9

May they possess all excellent qualities and favorable conditions, both worldly and transcendent, together with happiness, ^33-10

And peace and happiness of mind, and may they swiftly attain the supreme state of perfect Buddhahood. ^33-11

<small>Then recite the following stanza beginning, “Through the power of this great merit”:</small> ^33-12

Through the power of this great and powerful merit,
May the benefactors, together with all those connected with them,
Accomplish all their wishes in this very life,
May all obstacles be pacified, and may there be auspiciousness;
May there be the auspiciousness of all their wishes being accomplished in accordance with the Dharma. ^33-13

### Dedication for the Deceased ^33-2-0

All Buddhas and Bodhisattvas dwelling throughout the ten directions, please turn your compassionate attention toward the mindstream of the deceased. ^33-14

Whatever roots of virtue included within the two accumulations of merit and wisdom the deceased person named [name] has gathered from beginningless saṃsāra until the present moment, ^33-15

Together with all the roots of virtue that will be accomplished in future lives, ^33-16

And taking as representative these roots of virtue now accomplished for the sake of the deceased by the relatives and loved ones who remain behind, ^33-17

In short, gathering together as one all accumulations of virtue—both contaminated and uncontaminated— of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa, may they follow after the deceased and accompany them. ^33-18

Through the power and potency of all these virtues, may the deceased be freed and liberated from saṃsārain general and from every miserable state and rebirth in the lower realms in particular. ^33-19

As a completely pure basis, may they obtain a precious human body endowed with the freedoms and favorable conditions. ^33-20

As the perfect supporting condition, may they joyfully be accepted and guided by a fully qualified spiritual teacher. ^33-21

As the completely pure method, may they generate the supreme intention of bodhicitta. ^33-22

As the application of the path, may they have the good fortune to practice the six pāramitās properly. ^33-23

<small>If desired, one may elaborate here upon karma and its ripening results.</small> ^33-24

May everything that is to be abandoned—all obscurations of karma, afflictive emotions, and meditative absorption—without exception, be completely cleansed and purified. ^33-25

May all the immeasurable qualities of the paths and bhūmis be completely perfected. ^33-26

As the fruit, may they swiftly attain the precious state of perfect Buddhahood, whose nature is the enlightened kāyas and primordial wisdoms. ^33-27

In short, gathering together as one all accumulations of virtue, without exception—both contaminated and uncontaminated—of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa, ^33-28

And taking the deceased as the principal object of dedication, together with all sentient beings as limitless as space, we completely dedicate all these virtues as causes for their attainment of great enlightenment. May this dedication be accomplished exactly as intended. ^33-29

### Aspiration for the Deceased ^33-3-0

Without having to experience the sufferings of the lower realms,
And even without having to undergo severe hardships,
May they obtain a body even more excellent than that of the gods,
And through that, may they swiftly attain Buddhahood. ^33-30

Just as a lotus is unstained by mud,
May they remain unstained by the defilements of the three realms of existence.
May they be miraculously born from a lotus,
And take birth in Sukhāvatī, the Pure Land of Bliss. ^33-31

<small>Just as a drop of water falling into the great ocean Will never be exhausted until the ocean itself dries up, Likewise, whatever virtue is dedicated to enlightenment Will never be exhausted until enlightenment is attained. And the venerable Jetsun Milarepa said:The great meditator who practices in a mountain cave, And the benefactor who provides him with the necessities of life— There is an auspicious dependent connection for both to attain Buddhahood together. The essence of this dependent connection is the dedication of merit.</small> ^33-32

Through the virtue of the past, future, and present—
All the virtue accumulated throughout these three times—
May our parents and all beings of the six realms
Swiftly attain Buddhahood. ^33-33

<small>Spoken by Thangpa Rinpoche Tashi Pal. Sarva Maṅgalam— May all be auspicious!</small> ^33-34
