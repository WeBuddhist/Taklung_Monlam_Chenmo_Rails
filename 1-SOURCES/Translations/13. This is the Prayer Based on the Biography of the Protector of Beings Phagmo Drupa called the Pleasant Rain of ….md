# This is the Prayer Based on the Biography of the Protector of Beings Phagmo Drupa called the Pleasant Rain of the Amrita of Blessings ^0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-0]]

## This is the Prayer Based on the Biography of the Protector of Beings Phagmo Drupa called the Pleasant Rain of the Amrita of Blessings ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-1]]

Homage to the Guru! ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-2]]

Long ago, the Tathagata Krakucchanda (Destroyer of Samsara)
In this realm, the Completely Enligtened One, Shakyamuni
In the future, in the world “Covered with Flowers”
You take the form of Akshobhyavajra, and teach the Dharma,
I pray to the Precious Protector of Beings! ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-3]]

In the sight of the Buddha Unattached Lotus
the householder Wheel of Glory and so on,
the Eight Masters who attained the final limit of bodhicitta (the Tenth Bhumi)
learned and accomplished king’s ministers who manifest purified ordinariness,
I pray to the guide of limitless sentient beings! ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-4]]

Then, as the Regent of Youthful Moonlight,
in Do Kham, toward Dri Valley, by the Five Clairvoyances
manifesting the Nirmanakaya, without the suffering of birth,
in that very moment, clearly knowling the three times,
I pray to the Holy Supreme Nirmanakaya! ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-5]]

Arising from the ocean of innumerable Samadhis,
In front of the Kalyanamitra Blazing Ethics, and
Victory Banner of Light Rays, becoming ordained,
You became known as the Protector of Beings, Dorje Gyalpo
I pray to the one who wears the crown of the Signs of Perfection! ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-6]]

Rest from noise and labor, principal one of the many, and so on,
Not relying on training, naturally becoming omniscient,
from the Sixteen Spiritual Friends from Do Kham,
You listened to inconceivable authoritative texts and oral instructions,
I pray to the one who ripens and liberates the fortunate! ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-7]]

At the age of 22, you went towards U Tsang (Central Tibet)
and coming to the Dharma College Seat at the edge of Tolung,
You listened to many texts of Vinaya, Abhidharma, Madhyamaka, and Prajnaparamita,
Without an equal in teaching debate, exposition, and composition,
I pray to the Lord of the Learned in the Land of Snows! ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-8]]

At the Dharma College of Tsurphu, the great Tamer of Actions and so on,
from the masters and disciples of the Assembly of the Learned,
becoming fully ordained, holding the victory banner of the burial shroud,
and became famous as the second Buddha,
I pray to the one who beautifies this world! ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-9]]

From teachers of the Mahayana, Ja Yul and so on,
You took the vows of Aspiration and Application Bodhicitta,
Before the face of Tsechen Kunga Nyingpo
You listened to an ocean of mantra, tantra and oral instructions,
and manifested the warmth and signs of channels, nadis, and bindu,
I pray to the Great Vajra Holder! ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-10]]

The Bodhisattva Chandradhvaja, and
Tsami Lotsawa, Palchen Galo, and so on,
from innumerable learned and accomplished Masters,
all the Dharma precepts that exist in this Valley of Snows without exception,
were heard, contemplated, and meditated, ripening your mind stream,
I pray to the Great Learned and Accomplished One! ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-11]]

Prophesied by the Victor, peerless Gampopa’s
lotus feet were placed on your head, offering the three ways of pleasing,
You actually perceived the unmistaken truth of the Dharmata,
applying the elegant amrita of profound vast certainty,
You vowed to equal the Guru’s abandonment and realization.
I pray to You, Great Dharma King! ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-12]]

In pure vision, meeting the Holy Buddha and
the One Mother, andfilled like a vase with all the healing
oral instructions that could be obtained, without exception,
in the forty-ninth Male Earth Tiger Year,
in the forest solitude of Samantabhadra, you accomplished Varahi.
I pray to the one who abides on the Lion Throne! ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-13]]

Those who thirst for the amrita of Brahma’s speech,
fortunate disciples, in numbers beyond counting,
abide in the discipline of the amazing holy Dharma,
Surrounded by a throng of Noble Ones, who guard the precepts,
Taming and pacifying with the Bodhicitta of exchanging self and others,
I pray to the supreme Great Sattva! ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-14]]

Completely delighted by the vajra dance of great bliss,
attaining complete enlightenment by the royal samadhi of evenness,
pervading by continuous inexhaustible activity,
Gaining power over the ocean of innumerable Dharma treasures,
by the natural state of bliss and emptiness, Mahamudra,
I pray to the one who ripens and liberates the fortunate! ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-15]]

Of the twenty-five chief disciples, five hundred umbrella-bearers,
and an assembly of eight hundred, and others beyond number,
There is no one who was not liberated just by hearing the profound Dharma.
Heart essence of all the Wheel of Dharma,
with perfect words and perfect meaning, equal to space,
I bow down to those who composed the commentaries that protect the Dharma, ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-16]]

whose Three Secrets manifest inconceivable miracles,
and the smallest connection accomplishes great benefit for beings,
Guru who knows the capacities of each fortunate one,
I pray to the fully accomplished one,
who manifested twelve times 108,000 bodies. ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-17]]

Innumerable disciples of this place were liberated from the ocean of existence,
and in the sixty-first Tiger year,
on the twenty-fifth day of the ripening moon, your manifested Form Kaya
dissolved, and in the stainless pure land,
in the presence of the Buddha Akshobhya, attained enlightenment.
I bow down to LodroDrimay Gyalpo! ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-18]]

When your remains were purified, the Tathagatas of the Five Families appeared,
and the assembly of Dakas and Dakinis praised and made offerings,
heart, tongue and eye relics beyond imagination,
with earthquakes, rains of flowers, and massed rainbows,
a holy place with a Stupa of Dharmakaya was established.
I pray to the Inconceivable Miracle! ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-19]]

Thus, by seeing, hearing, and remembering this meaningful
King of Dharma, the great protector of beings,
if one prays while keeping your life story in mind,
from the equanimity of the Dharmata, great loving ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-20]]

compassion, like masses of clouds,
for those who follow your teachings, Protector,
on the fertile ground of the mine and other disciples’ mind streams,
Please allow the gentle rain of the amrita of blessings to quickly fall,
and ripen the perfect harvest of the Four Kayas of Great Bliss! ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-21]]

King of Dharma, in your Dharma Assembly,
overpowering hordes of Maras who hold malicious wrong views,
clearing away all outer, inner and secret obstacles, without exception,
please increase the ocean of those disciples who rest equally
in the definitive meaning of the natural state of bliss- emptiness! ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-22]]

In all countries, may the continuity of seasons be peaceful,
may everyone, everywhere, practice the path of the Ten Virtues as one,
And may all together quickly attain
the Holy State of the Vajra King! ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/13. འགྲོ་མགོན་ཕག་མོ་གྲུ་པའི་རྣམ་ཐར་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་བདུད་རྩིའི་སྦྲང་ཆར་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-23]]

<small>So, the Vajra Master, named Ayu, of the Great Dharma College, DrupgyuTengye Ling, that holds the Long Tradition of Definitive Meaning, requested me, Trinlay Jampa Jungne, who has received a small amount of blessing from the Venerable Guru, the Great Protector of Beings, to write this. May it be excellent virtue!</small> ^1-23
