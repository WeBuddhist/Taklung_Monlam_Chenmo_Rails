# Refuge, Bodhicitta, Invocation, Blessing the Foundation, and so on. ^0

## Refuge, Bodhicitta, Invocation, Blessing the Foundation, and so on. ^1-0

I and all sentient beings as limitless as the sky go for refuge to the essence of all the Tathagatas of the three times and the ten directions’ body, speech, mind, qualities and activity, the source of all the 84,000 collections of Dharma, ^1-1

the lord of all the noble Sangha, to the the all the glorious, compassionate, root and lineage Gurus! ^1-2

I go for refuge to all the assembled mandalas of the Yidams! ^1-3

I go for refuge to all Buddhas, the accomplished transcendant conquerors! ^1-4

I go for refuge to the holy Dharma! ^1-5

I go for refuge to all the noble Sangha! ^1-6

I go for refuge to all Dakas, Dakinis, Dharmapalas and Guards endowed with the eyes of original awareness! ^1-7

To the Buddha, Dharma, and all the Supreme Assembly,
I go for refuge until Enlightenment.
By the merit of my generosity and so forth,
My I attain Buddhahood for the benefit of beings. ^1-8

May all sentient beings be endowed with happiness and the causes of happiness! ^1-9

May they be free from suffering and the causes of suffering! ^1-10

May they never be separated from the sacred bliss that is free from suffering! ^1-11

May they abide in the great equanimity free of attachment and aversion to near and far! ^1-12

By the truth of the Three Rare and Supreme Ones, and ^1-13

by the power of all the completely pure Buddha Realms of Buddhas and Bodhisattvas who have completed the Pure Realm training and so on, ^1-14

by the power of our devotion, may this place be adorned with all the Buddha Realms and perfectly endowed with all qualities! ^1-15

May the ground be pure everywhere,
with no pebbles or sharp objects,
level like the palm of the hand,
naturally smooth and composed of Lapis Lazuli. ^1-16

May pleasure groves of wish-fulfilling trees,
with all the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas,
filled with the sweet sound of the Dharma,
pervade everywhere in all directions! ^1-17

Although the surrounding mandala may decline,
along with many pure Bodhisattvas,
may my own excellent qualities
continually abide as a beautiful ornament! ^1-18

May all embodied beings, birds and so forth,
the pure realm and all the rays of light,
extending even beyond space, continually resound
with the unceasing song of Dharma! ^1-19

HUNG! Protecting all sentient beings without exception,
Unconquered Deity who destroys the heaped race of Maras,
Who knows all Reality just as it is,
Please come to this place with your retinue, Accomplished Conqueror! ^1-20

Bhagavan, for many countless aeons,
Purifying compassion out of love for beings,
Completely perfecting your ancient vast aspiration,
Because of your intention to benefit all beings, ^1-21

from the natural palace of the Dharmakaya,
bestowing blessings and displaying manifold miracles,
so that all sentient beings may be ultimately liberated,
Total Purity, together with your retinue, please come here! ^1-22

Lord, who is powerful over all Dharmas,
shining like refined pure gold,
with vast radiance greater than the sun,
by sincerely gazing with the eyes of faith, ^1-23

peaceful, endowed with great compassion,
disciplined, abiding in the meditative states,
free of attachment to phenomena and awareness,
all endowed with unconquerable strength, ^1-24

Come here! Come here, you deities of this place!
Omniscient supreme conqueror,
Reflected image of extremely virtuous deeds,
Please come to this place of offerings! ^1-25

Here and now, together with your form,
We pray that you not only abide for the benefit of beings,
but bestow power over lifespan and freedom from illness,
and bestow every excellence! ^1-26

Compassionate conqueror, it is excellent that you have come!
We are all endowed with merit and great fortune!
Along with accepting my offering substances,
Please abide here and now! ^1-27

In the pollen heart of an eight-petalled lotus,
the size of all the billionfold universe,
expanding the offerings with bliss,
we pray you will blissfully abide here! ^1-28

Just as when the Buddha was born,
the gods offered him a bath,
and purified Him with divine water,
in that same way, I offer ablution, ^1-29

with this supreme purifying substance,
the unsurpassable water of compassion.
By the blessings of this water of pristine awareness,
Please bestow the accomplishment of whatever is desired! ^1-30

I invite all the Tathagatas and their Bodhisattva children:
Come bathe yourselves in scented water,
poured from overflowing vases made of beautiful jewels,
all while accompanied by beautiful music! ^1-31

Although the Buddha’s body, speech and mind are not obscured with afflictive emotions,
In order to purify the stains of sentient beings’ body, speech and mind,
by the Buddha’s accepting this bathing of their body, speech and mind,
May the stains of sentient beings’ body, speech and mind be purified! ^1-32

Then let me dry you with incomparable cloth,
Pure and anointed with beautiful perfume,
and dress you in the most excellent of garments,
lightly scented and dyed in vivid colours, ^1-33

I offer clothing made of the finest fabrics,
and hundreds of the most beautiful ornaments,
to grace the bodies of noble Samantabhadra, Manjughosha,
Lokeshvara and all the rest, ^1-34

with the softest and most luxurious divine raiment!
Through my offering to your pure unchangeable vajra
body with undivided faith,
may I also attain the vajra body! ^1-35

These clothes, multicolored like a rainbow,
that produce bliss whenever they are touched,
I offer in order to purify my own mind.
May I be adorned with the sublime clothes of patience! ^1-36

May a multitude of other offerings,
with sweet music, beautiful to hear,
be arrayed in great successive clouds
to dispel the sufferings of sentient beings! ^1-37
