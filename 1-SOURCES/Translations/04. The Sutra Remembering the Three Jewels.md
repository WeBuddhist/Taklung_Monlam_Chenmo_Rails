# The Sutra Remembering the Three Jewels ^0

## The Sutra Remembering the Three Jewels ^1-0

In the language of India: aryaratnatraya anu smriti sutra ^1-1

In the language of Tibet: ‘phags pa dkonmchoggsumrjessudranpa’imdo ^1-2

I prostrate to the Three Jewels! ^1-3

Thus, the Buddha, the accomplished transcendent conqueror, the tathagata who has attained thatness, the arhat who has conquered all foes, is a perfectly and completely enlightened buddha, ^1-4

endowed with awareness and worthy of reverence, ^1-5

the Sugata who has gone to bliss, ^1-6

knower of the entire world, ^1-7

guide and tamer of beings, ^1-8

unsurpassable, ^1-9

teacher of gods and humans, the Buddha, accomplished transcendent conqueror, ^1-10

that Tathagata is compatible with the causes of pure merit, ^1-11

he does not waste the sources of virtue, ^1-12

he is supremely adorned with patience, ^1-13

he is the foundation of all the treasures of merit, ^1-14

adorned with all the excellent minor marks, ^1-15

and the full flowering of the major marks, ^1-16

with timely and appropriate activity, ^1-17

with nothing displeasing to see, ^1-18

He brings complete joy to those who have faith and devotion, ^1-19

He has unconquerable wisdom, ^1-20

His powers are invincible, ^1-21

He is the teacher of all sentient beings, ^1-22

the father of all bodhisattvas, ^1-23

the king of all noble beings, ^1-24

the guide who leads all beings to the city of transcending sorrow, ^1-25

with measureless original awareness, ^1-26

with inconceivable fearlessness, ^1-27

with completely pure speech ^1-28

in melodious tones, ^1-29

one is never tired of seeing him, ^1-30

his embodied form is incomparable, ^1-31

unobscured by the realm of desire, ^1-32

unclothed by the realm of form, ^1-33

not adulterated with the formless realm, ^1-34

completely liberated from all suffering, ^1-35

totally liberated from all the aggregates, ^1-36

not encumbered by the elements, ^1-37

in complete control of the sense processes, ^1-38

He has completely severed all the knots, ^1-39

He is completely liberated from all agony, ^1-40

He is liberated from existence, ^1-41

he has crossed over the river, ^1-42

He has completely perfected original awareness ^1-43

(abiding in) all the past and future ^1-44

and present Buddha Bhagavan’s original awareness, ^1-45

not abiding in Nirvana, ^1-46

He abides at the limit of perfection itself, ^1-47

abiding on the level where all sentient beings are seen, ^1-48

all these are the authentic and supreme qualities of the embodied form of the Buddha Bhagavan. ^1-49

The holy Dharma is good at the beginning, good in the middle and good at the end, ^1-50

It is excellent in meaning, in words, and in syllables, ^1-51

It is unadulterated, utterly complete, absolutely pure, and absolutely purifying, ^1-52

it is taught perfectly by the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror. ^1-53

it brings perfect vision, ^1-54

It is unafflicted, uninterrupted, trustworthy to apply, ^1-55

encountering it fulfills all aspirations, ^1-56

It is validated through the individual experience of the wise, ^1-57

the Dharma taught by the Bhagavan consists entirely on taming the mind, ^1-58

it is the arising of certainty, ^1-59

causing one to arrive at complete enlightenment. ^1-60

It is without contradiction, including everything, ^1-61

stable and unmoving, ^1-62

as for the Mahayana Sangha, they abide excellently, ^1-63

they abide with awareness, abide with honesty, abide in harmony, ^1-64

They are worthy of veneration with folded hands, ^1-65

worthy of veneration by prostration, ^1-66

they are a glorious field of merit, ^1-67

offering to them brings great purification, ^1-68

they are an object for generosity, ^1-69

they are absolutely the greatest object for generosity. ^1-70

The Buddha’s qualities are inconceivable.
The Dharma’s qualities are inconceivable.
The Noble Sangha is inconceivable.
By having confidence in the inconceivable, ^1-71

complete liberation is also inconceivable!
May we be born in the completely pure realm! ^1-72

<small>The Sutra of Remembering the Noble Three Jewels is complete.</small> ^1-73
