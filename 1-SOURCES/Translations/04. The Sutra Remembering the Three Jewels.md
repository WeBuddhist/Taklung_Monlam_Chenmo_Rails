# The Sutra Remembering the Three Jewels ^4-0

In the language of India: aryaratnatraya anu smriti sutra ^4-1

In the language of Tibet: ‘phags pa dkonmchoggsumrjessudranpa’imdo ^4-2

I prostrate to the Three Jewels! ^4-3

Thus, the Buddha, the accomplished transcendent conqueror, the tathagata who has attained thatness, the arhat who has conquered all foes, is a perfectly and completely enlightened buddha, ^4-4

endowed with awareness and worthy of reverence, ^4-5

the Sugata who has gone to bliss, ^4-6

knower of the entire world, ^4-7

guide and tamer of beings, ^4-8

unsurpassable, ^4-9

teacher of gods and humans, the Buddha, accomplished transcendent conqueror, ^4-10

that Tathagata is compatible with the causes of pure merit, ^4-11

he does not waste the sources of virtue, ^4-12

he is supremely adorned with patience, ^4-13

he is the foundation of all the treasures of merit, ^4-14

adorned with all the excellent minor marks, ^4-15

and the full flowering of the major marks, ^4-16

with timely and appropriate activity, ^4-17

with nothing displeasing to see, ^4-18

He brings complete joy to those who have faith and devotion, ^4-19

He has unconquerable wisdom, ^4-20

His powers are invincible, ^4-21

He is the teacher of all sentient beings, ^4-22

the father of all bodhisattvas, ^4-23

the king of all noble beings, ^4-24

the guide who leads all beings to the city of transcending sorrow, ^4-25

with measureless original awareness, ^4-26

with inconceivable fearlessness, ^4-27

with completely pure speech ^4-28

in melodious tones, ^4-29

one is never tired of seeing him, ^4-30

his embodied form is incomparable, ^4-31

unobscured by the realm of desire, ^4-32

unclothed by the realm of form, ^4-33

not adulterated with the formless realm, ^4-34

completely liberated from all suffering, ^4-35

totally liberated from all the aggregates, ^4-36

not encumbered by the elements, ^4-37

in complete control of the sense processes, ^4-38

He has completely severed all the knots, ^4-39

He is completely liberated from all agony, ^4-40

He is liberated from existence, ^4-41

he has crossed over the river, ^4-42

He has completely perfected original awareness ^4-43

(abiding in) all the past and future ^4-44

and present Buddha Bhagavan’s original awareness, ^4-45

not abiding in Nirvana, ^4-46

He abides at the limit of perfection itself, ^4-47

abiding on the level where all sentient beings are seen, ^4-48

all these are the authentic and supreme qualities of the embodied form of the Buddha Bhagavan. ^4-49

The holy Dharma is good at the beginning, good in the middle and good at the end, ^4-50

It is excellent in meaning, in words, and in syllables, ^4-51

It is unadulterated, utterly complete, absolutely pure, and absolutely purifying, ^4-52

it is taught perfectly by the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror. ^4-53

it brings perfect vision, ^4-54

It is unafflicted, uninterrupted, trustworthy to apply, ^4-55

encountering it fulfills all aspirations, ^4-56

It is validated through the individual experience of the wise, ^4-57

the Dharma taught by the Bhagavan consists entirely on taming the mind, ^4-58

it is the arising of certainty, ^4-59

causing one to arrive at complete enlightenment. ^4-60

It is without contradiction, including everything, ^4-61

stable and unmoving, ^4-62

as for the Mahayana Sangha, they abide excellently, ^4-63

they abide with awareness, abide with honesty, abide in harmony, ^4-64

They are worthy of veneration with folded hands, ^4-65

worthy of veneration by prostration, ^4-66

they are a glorious field of merit, ^4-67

offering to them brings great purification, ^4-68

they are an object for generosity, ^4-69

they are absolutely the greatest object for generosity. ^4-70

The Buddha’s qualities are inconceivable.
The Dharma’s qualities are inconceivable.
The Noble Sangha is inconceivable.
By having confidence in the inconceivable, ^4-71

complete liberation is also inconceivable!
May we be born in the completely pure realm! ^4-72

<small>The Sutra of Remembering the Noble Three Jewels is complete.</small> ^4-73
