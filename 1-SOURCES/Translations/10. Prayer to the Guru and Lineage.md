# Prayer to the Guru and Lineage ^0

## Prayer to the Guru and Lineage ^1-0

Whose kindness is Great Bliss itself,
instantly shining forth and filling the world,
Guru’s embodied form, like a jewel,
I bow down to your vajra lotus feet! ^1-1

Unchanging great bliss with illusory form,
unified with the Wisdom Consort,
glory of all conditioned existence and Nirvana,
I prostrate to powerful Vajradhara! ^1-2

You who, like extracting the oil from sesame,
Are great Original Awareness abiding in a body,
Principal Lord of Dakinis, excellently seeing,
I prostrate to Venerable Tilopa! ^1-3

Without being discouraged by all difficulties,
You, Learned One, in an instant
were completely liberated by the highest path,
I prostrate to Glorious Naropa! ^1-4

Supremely abiding in the ocean of the Noble Land,
Mahasiddha who relied with devotion on the King of Nagas (Buddha),
Possessing the jewel treasury of the Hearing Lineage,
I prostrate to Marpa the Translator! ^1-5

By the strong earthquake of diligent accomplishment,
obtaining supreme complete realization,
Master of all the Lords of Accomplished Ones,
I prostrate to Laughing Vajra (Milarepa)! ^1-6

Holder of the prophecy of the Lion of the Shakyas,
By disciplined hearing and thinking,
holding the victory banner of the teachings of the Practice Lineage,
I prostrate to Youthful Moonlight (Gampopa)! ^1-7

Entering the heart of reasoning and scriptural authority,
completely liberated by supreme realization,
Holding all of the Guide’s Holy Dharma,
I prostrate to Dorje Gyalpo (Phagmodrupa) ! ^1-8

Supremely ornamented by accumulated ethical conduct,
Without any rivals or equals
in attaining the mastery of samadhi,
I prostrate to Taklung Tangpa! ^1-9

Combining the rivers of the Kadampa and Mahamudra,
perfectly holding the tradition of the Charioteers,
You became the protector of all beings,
I prostrate to Ratnanatha! ^1-10

Completely liberated from all faults,
perfectly adorned with the ornaments of ethical conduct,
Holding the victory banner of unending accomplishment,
I prostrate to the Wisdom Guru! ^1-11

Fully manifest omniscient Original Awareness,
You became a treasure of nonconceptual love
through an unending flow of enlightened activity,
I prostrate to the auspicious Guru! ^1-12

Learned in all objects of knowledge,
supremely accomplishing complete liberation,
clarifying the doctrines of scripture and realization,
I prostrate to Sangye Palzang! ^1-13

Purifying all the latent tendencies of the two obscurations,
treasure of all the qualities of realization,
limitless power and enlightened activity,
I prostrate to Ratna Guru! ^1-14

Protector, the excellent vase of your heart,
filled with the purifying water of omniscient love,
ripening and liberating all beings,
I prostrate to Ratnakara! ^1-15

In the traditions of the authoritative teachings of sutra and tantra,
Perfectly completing all hearing and thinking,
diligently accomplishing his own original state,
I prostrate to the Great One who has renounced everything! ^1-16

Essence of all the Buddhas of the three times,
Treasury of the 84,000 Dharmas,
Leader of the ocean of the supreme noble Sangha,
I prostrate to the mass of auspicious glory! ^1-17

You are the unity of all acts of omniscient love
by all Buddhas without exception,
the only friend of all living beings,
I prostrate to the one with the signs of enlightenment! ^1-18

Steadfast in all the roots of merit,
You touch the sky at the peak of Original Awareness,
Wish-fulfilling tree, rainfall of all things desired,
I prostrate to the light of auspicious glory! ^1-19

Whatever excellent teachings there may be
are perfectly summarized by his discerning intelligence,
the stainless teachings are made more excellent.
I prostrate to the Lord of Speech! ^1-20

Making all auspiciousness more auspicious,
Excellently ornamented by all glory,
To all those of great renown,
I praise you with supreme reverence! ^1-21

The light rays of your embodied form
are supremely victorious over all conflicting conditions,
and renowned for the assembly of disciples’ supportive conditions
I prostrate to the guiding Lord of Dharma! ^1-22

In the centre of the vast ocean of merit
filled with the glorious jewels of the Kagyu,
move the successive waves of perfect Activity,
I prostrate to the Guru of living beings! ^1-23

By the compassionate power of the completely omniscient Victors,
Wonderful auspicious Nirmanakaya
holding the victory banner of ancient renown,
I prostrate to the perfect glorious Protector! ^1-24

Treasure of many Victors’ omniscient love,
by the celebration of perfect excellent teachings,
supremely bestowing ripening and liberation on those to be tamed,
I prostrate to the Holy Master! ^1-25

Glorious joyful bliss of any and all beings,
skillfully sending a rainfall of auspicious Dharma,
You cause the sprouts of complete liberation to grow,
I prostrate to the great heroic being! ^1-26

Pervading Lord, vajra body of great bliss,
unconquerable glory of all conditioned existence and peace,
with eternal and permanent Buddha Activity,
I prostrate to Ngawang Namgyal! ^1-27

Treasury of the ocean of eloquent supreme teachings,
glorious doctrine of the auspicious Practice Lineage,
with wonderful spontaneously accomplished activity,
I prostrate to Choktrul Dampa! ^1-28

Gloriously filling the ocean of the Two Accumulations,
perfect accumulation of the ethics of Original Awareness,
Only protector of all beings, including the gods,
I prostrate to DrakpaLekdrup! ^1-29

Completely destroying great heaps of the class of Maras,
Pacifying all afflicted concepts, without exception,
Completely filling the mandala of the wise,
I prostrate to Jampal Gyatso! ^1-30

Natural embodiment of all Victors’ compassion,
Supreme banner of both temporal and spiritual traditions,
Directly lifting up this conditioned world,
I prostrate to Ngawang Drakpa! ^1-31

Filling the minds of both sutra and mantra,
treasure of many glorious qualities,
you bestow all ordinary and supreme accomplishments
I prostrate to Trinlay Tendzin! ^1-32

Holder of the white lotus, protector of beings,
who is like the Second Buddha
Great body, the only glory of benefit and happiness,
I prostrate to Drakpa Rinchen! ^1-33

From the ocean of previous training in the two accumulations,
By the wish-fulfilling jewel of great love,
You clear away all the poverty of Samsara and Nirvana
I prostrate to NyendrakPalzang! ^1-34

You perceive with no delusion the natural state as it is,
Knowing all objects of knowledge as they appear,
Naturally benefitting self and others,
I prostrate to Rinchen Lekdrup! ^1-35

In the ocean of countless Victorious Ones,
You combine the three qualities of omniscience, love, and ability in one,
Incomparable spiritual friend of this Doctrine,
I prostrate to ChoyingLhundrup! ^1-36

Here and now, the essential teachings
among all beings of the ten directions with their families,
blaze with fame in the realm of ordinary beings,
I prostrate to Chokyi Paljor! ^1-37

Supreme victory banner of the doctrines of scripture and realization,
Is completely, supremely, and permanently raised
at the pinnacle of conditioned existence,
I prostrate to Drakpa Gyatso! ^1-38

All clouds of acts of omniscient,
powerful love of the Victors of the three times with their heirs,
Swirl like a cloak around the glory of your mind.
I prostrate to DrakpaKunsal! ^1-39

The hundred thousand suns of omniscient love
clear away the darkness of ignorance of the Three Worlds
With the glorious quality of naturally accomplishing the two benefits,
I prostrate to LodroDrimay! ^1-40

The three secrets of all Buddhas
are gathered into one in your own body itself.
Appearing in the form of Vajradhara,
I prostrate to KungayTsenchen! ^1-41

Treasury of an ocean of the qualities of renunciation and realization,
with Enlightened Activity for the benefit of others
Treasury of an ocean of Dharma traditions of scripture and realization,
I prostrate to Ngawang Lekdrup! ^1-42

May I, from this time forward,
Until the essence of Enlightenment is reached,
To the Guru, the embodiment of all Buddhas,
and all the assembled ones, I go for refuge! ^1-43

Clouds of outer, inner and secret offerings
My own body and also my enjoyments,
Whatever virtue I’ve accumulated in the three times,
I offer all of that to all of you! ^1-44

From beginningless Samsara,
under the power of ignorant disrespect and
all heedless emotional afflictions,
I free admit and lay aside all faults! ^1-45

The supreme Guru and Victors, and
the Victors’ heirs (Bodhisattvas) and Pratyekabuddhas,
Sravakas, and ordinary people,
I rejoice in all of their virtue! ^1-46

For those with lesser intelligence who have difficulty realizing,
who don’t understand the teachings of Dharma,
may all the Guides and myself,
turn the wheel of Dharma! ^1-47

For as long as Samsara exists,
not passing into Nirvana, but remaining forever,
Accomplishing benefit for myself and all others,
I pray to you with sincere intention! ^1-48

Just as all the Victors
and their heirs have completely dedicated
This virtue, and all other virtues,
I dedicate to the great enlightenment of all beings! ^1-49

<small>Thus, this prayer in verse form to all the Kagyu Rinpoches was written with a devoted mind by the BhikhshuNgaggi Wangpo. From Ngawang Namgyal on down, the lineage prayers were previously amended by others.</small> ^1-50
