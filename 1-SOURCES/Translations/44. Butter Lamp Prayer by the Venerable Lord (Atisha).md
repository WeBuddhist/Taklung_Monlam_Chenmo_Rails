# Butter Lamp Prayer by the Venerable Lord (Atisha) ^0

## Butter Lamp Prayer by the Venerable Lord (Atisha) ^44-0

Butter Lamp Prayer spoken by the Peerless Venerable Lord, Glorious Atisha ^44-1

May this butter lamp vessel become as vast as the billionfold universe! ^44-2

May this wick grow as large as the Supreme King of Mountains! ^44-3

May the butter oil become as vast as the ocean at the edge of the world! ^44-4

May a billion of these lamps appear before each and every Buddha! ^44-5

Their light shining from the pinnacle of existence down ^44-6

to the lowest hell, clearly revealing all the realms of Buddhas and Bodhisattvas in the ten directions! ^44-7

OM VAJRA ALOKE A HUNG. ^44-8

Emaho! This wonderful and amazing light, burning brightly, ^44-9

I offer to the thousand buddhas of this fortunate age,
and to all the infinite realms of the ten directions, without exception,
to the Gurus, Yidams, Dakinis and Dharmapalas,
and all the deities of all the mandalas, I make this offering! ^44-10

May all beings, particularly my own parents,
in this life and in all lives to come, wherever we are born,
directly perceive all the perfect buddha realms
and remain inseparable from the Lord of Boundless Light (Amitabha)! ^44-11

And by the power of truth of this aspiration prayer
to the Three Jewels and all the assembled deities of the Three Roots,
May this prayer be quickly accomplished! ^44-12

TADYATHA PANCHENDRIYA AVABODHANAYE SVAHA. ^44-13

<small>It is said that the Venerable Lord Glorious Atisha and seventeen disciples would chant this together as they made offerings in the shrines of Ü and Tsang.</small> ^44-14
