## The Heart of Wisdom ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-1]]

Indescribable, inconceivable, inexpressible Transcendent Wisdom
unborn and unceasing, the very nature of space,
the realm of your own self-knowing original awareness;
I pay homage to the mother of the Buddhas of the three times! ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-2]]

In the language of India: Bhagavati Prajnaparamita Hridaya ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-3]]

In the language of Tibet: Chom Den Day Ma Sherab kyi Parol tuChinpayNyingpo ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-4]]

I prostrate to the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueress Transcendent Wisdom! ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-5]]

Thus have I heard, at one time, ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-6]]

The Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror was dwelling in Rajagriha at Vulture Peak Mountain, ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-7]]

together with a great gathering of the community of Bhikshus ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-8]]

and a great gathering of the community of Bodhisattvas. ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-9]]

At that time, the Glorious One entered the meditation that expresses the reality called “Profound Illumination,” ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-10]]

and at the same time, noble Avalokiteshvara, the Great Being, the Bodhisattva, while practicing the profound Transcendent Wisdom, saw in this way: ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-11]]

he saw the five aggregates to be naturally empty. ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-12]]

Then, through the power of the Buddha, venerable Shariputra ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-13]]

said to noble Avalokiteshvara, the Bodhisattva Mahasattva, ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-14]]

“How should a son or daughter of noble family learn, who wishes to practice the profound Transcendent Wisdom?” ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-15]]

Addressed in this way, ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-16]]

noble Avalokiteshvara, the Bodhisattva Mahasattva, said to venerable Shariputra, ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-17]]

“O Shariputra, a son or daughter of noble family who wishes to practice the profound Transcendent Wisdom should see in this way: ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-18]]

seeing the five aggregates to be naturally empty. ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-19]]

Form is empty, Emptiness also is form. ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-20]]

Emptiness is no other than form, ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-21]]

Form is no other than emptiness. ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-22]]

In the same way, feeling, intention, and perception, ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-23]]

consciousness are all empty. ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-24]]

Thus, Shariputra, all phenomena are empty. ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-25]]

There are no characteristics, there is no birth ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-26]]

and no cessation. ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-27]]

There is no impurity and no freedom from impurity ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-28]]

There is no decrease and no increase. ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-29]]

Therefore, Shariputra, in emptiness, there is no form, ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-30]]

no feeling, no perception, no intention, ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-31]]

no eye, no ear, no nose, ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-32]]

no tongue, no body, no mind, ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-33]]

no appearance, no sound, no smell, ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-34]]

no taste, no touch, no phenomena. ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-35]]

no eye sense faculty up to no mind sense faculty, ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-36]]

no consciousness, ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-37]]

no awareness of phenomena, no mind consciousness faculty, ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-38]]

no ignorance, no end of ignorance up to no old age and death, ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-39]]

no end of old age and death. ^1-39

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-40]]

In that way there is no suffering, no cessation of suffering, no origin of suffering, ^1-40

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-41]]

no path, no Original Awareness, and no non-attainment either. no attainment, ^1-41

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-42]]

Therefore, Shariputra, since the Bodhisattvas have no attainment, they abide by means of Transcendent Wisdom.Since there is no obscuration of mind, there is no fear. Transcending false views, they pass completely beyond sorrow. ^1-42

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-43]]

All the Buddhas of the three times, by means of profound Transcendent Wisdom, fully awaken to unsurpassable, true, complete Enlightenment. ^1-43

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-44]]

Therefore, the great mantra of Transcendent Wisdom, ^1-44

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-45]]

the mantra of great insight, the unsurpassed mantra, ^1-45

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-46]]

the mantra that makes all inequalities equal, ^1-46

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-47]]

the mantra that supremely pacifies all suffering, ^1-47

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-48]]

should be known as truth, since there is no deception. ^1-48

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-49]]

The mantra of Transcendent Wisdom is said in this way: ^1-49

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-50]]

TADYATHA OM GATE GATE PARAGATE PARASAMGATE BODHI SVAHA. ^1-50

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-51]]

Thus, Shariputra, should the Bodhisattva Mahasattva engage in the profound Transcendent Wisdom. ^1-51

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-52]]

Then the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror arose from that meditation ^1-52

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-53]]

and praised noble Avalokiteshvara, the Bodhisattva Mahasattva, saying, ^1-53

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-54]]

“Excellent! Excellent!” O son of noble family, thus it is. O son of noble family! Thus it is, ^1-54

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-55]]

One should practice the profound Transcendent Wisdom just as you have taught, ^1-55

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-56]]

and all the Tathagatas will rejoice.” ^1-56

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-57]]

When the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror had said this, ^1-57

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-58]]

Venerable Shariputra and ^1-58

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-59]]

noble powerful Avalokiteshvara, the Bodhisattva Mahasattva, and ^1-59

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-60]]

and that whole assembly, and ^1-60

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-61]]

the world with its gods, humans, demigods, and spirits, all rejoiced and praised the words of the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror. ^1-61

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-62]]

<small>Then recite seven times or as many as possible, then, after reciting the awareness mantra as many times as possible:</small> ^1-62

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-63]]

I prostrate to the Dharma! Namo! I prostrate to the Guru! I prostrate to the Buddha! ^1-63

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-64]]

I prostrate to the Sangha! I prostrate to the Great Mother Transcendent Wisdom! ^1-64

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-65]]

May these words of mine come true! ^1-65

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-66]]

Just as, long ago, Indra, the King of the Gods, by the power and strength of contemplating the profound meaning of Transcendent Wisdom and reciting its words, ^1-66

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-67]]

was able to avert all harmful forces of negativity and obstacles, so, in the very same way, ^1-67

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-68]]

May I also, by the power and strength of my contemplating the profound meaning of Transcendent Wisdom, and reciting its words: May all negative influences be averted! * ^1-68

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-69]]

May they be destroyed! * May they be pacified! * ^1-69

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-70]]

May they be completely pacified! * ^1-70

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-71]]

Everything that arises interdependently
is unceasing and unborn,
neither non-existent or permanent,
neither arising or passing away, ^1-71

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-72]]

without a single meaning or several meanings.
All elaborations and explanations are pacified.
To the sacred words of the Perfect Buddha,
To that Holy One, I bow down! ^1-72

![[1-SOURCES/Text/35. ཤེས་རབ་སྙིང་པོ.md#^1-73]]

Pacifying the eighty-four thousand classes of obstructors,
Separating from all harmful conflicting conditions,
Accomplishing all desires with everything perfectly in place,
May everything be auspicious excellent bliss! ^1-73
