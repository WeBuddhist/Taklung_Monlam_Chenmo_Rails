# Praise of the Sage ^0

## Praise of the Sage ^7-0

Homage to Shakyamuni! ^7-1

Because He is the glory of the world and nirvana,
Pinnacle of the Shakyas, happily accomplished;
To that guide for all beings of the three realms,
I prostrate with supreme reverence! ^7-2

On the page of the joyful Kshatriya caste,
brought forth with the writing brush of merit
the excellently drawn pictures on his soles,
accomplishing benefit and happiness through the appearance of pure qualities, ^7-3

dependent on limitless compassion,
Your Body powerful like the king of elephants,
naturally uncontaminated like the great ocean,
being supported for a full ten months, ^7-4

effortlessly, from his mother’s hip,
He moved and naturally came forth
Beautiful, under a wish-fulfilling tree,
a tree completely rich with signs and examples, ^7-5

a field of knowledge and the arts,
clearer and brighter than the light-giving sun,
for those malicious adversaries who are completely corrupted,
evil spirit birds and malicious spirits, ^7-6

with all their sixty thousand alluring wives,
even offering the five sensual pleasures,
like a lotus blooming in the wilderness of a foul swamp,
never clothed by conditioned existence, ^7-7

having crossed the river of the Victors’ children,
departing to the completely pure stupa,
Your Body is the golden Mount Sumeru,
Your upper robe, the colour of the sunrise, embraces you like a maiden, ^7-8

Brahmin Rishis, without exception
unable to follow his unique example,
by the power of austerities, difficult to bear,
attaining the bliss of twenty-four factors, ^7-9

at the summit of the Vajra Seat,
on a mat of blue-green grass,
supremely abiding in the unmovable vajra posture,
By his power, like ten million hurricanes, ^7-10

unwilling Mara’s deluded mind,
all the many distorted manifestations
of Mara’s desires
are destroyed by the samadhi of love. ^7-11

Skillfully piloting the ship of meditation
on the great ocean of samsara, difficult to measure,
although he can cross over in an instant,
He has become the guru of the three worlds, ^7-12

With the wheel of the One Born from a Golden Egg (Brahma).
By praying with devotion in Your presence,
with five Bhikshus in the centre of a circle of Devas,
You beat the royal drum of the Dharma of the Four Truths, ^7-13

for Sattvas endowed with tenfold power, and
Bull of Speech, with an assembly of Sravakas,
who offered a fertile field of devotion and virtue,
with a rainfall of the Dharma of selflessness, ^7-14

staying happily in the beautiful house of the Three Yanas,
all those who abide will be henceforth taken under care,
and finally, opening everything excellently,
You supremely turned the Wheel of Dharma. ^7-15

The lotus garden of disciples, without exception,
are all protected by the sunlight of compassion. Then,
between two Sal trees,
You manifested passing into Nirvana. ^7-16

Your incomparable relics,
like the fabulous royal wish-granting gem,
become a lamp for the merit of this destructible world of gods
and human beings. ^7-17

Finally, as long as the world exists,
by manifesting a display of emanations,
and ripening innumerable disciples,
I continually bow down to your enlightened activity! ^7-18

By the power of praying in this way,
reaching the river crossing of your teachings,
May I, along with all who become your disciples,
attain the state of omniscience! ^7-19

<small>So, in order to benefit and bring joy to sincere students, the all-knowing Lord of Speech spoke this on the Holy Day of Sravana, on the twelfth day of the waxing moon, and written down by Ngawang Namgyal at the Great Vihara of the Glorious Taklung. Sarva Mangalam!</small> ^7-20
