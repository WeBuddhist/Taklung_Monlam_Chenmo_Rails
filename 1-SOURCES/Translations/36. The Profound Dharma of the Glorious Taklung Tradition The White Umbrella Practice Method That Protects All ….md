# The Profound Dharma of the Glorious Taklung Tradition: The White Umbrella Practice Method That Protects All Beings ^0

## The Profound Dharma of the Glorious Taklung Tradition: The White Umbrella Practice Method That Protects All Beings ^1-0

Homage to the Victorious, Auspicious Glory! ^1-1

Protecting all beings from the sufferings of samsaric existence,
And granting the cooling shade of complete liberation,
To the supreme Guru and Mother White Umbrella,
Who are inseparable, I pay homage. ^1-2

<small>Refuge</small> ^1-3

I instantly transform into the Lord of Sages (Buddha Shakyamuni), golden in color, ablaze with the glorious major and minor marks. ^1-4

His right hand touches the earth (bhumisparsha), His left rests in meditative poise (dhyana). ^1-5

He sits in the vajra posture upon a lotus and moon seat. ^1-6

Wearing the three saffron robes, his light fills all world-realms. ^1-7

From his crown protuberance (usnisa), a white OṀ radiates into the sky before me, ^1-8

resting upon a beautiful mandala of a lotus and moon disk. ^1-9

Light rays emanate from it, making offerings to the Noble Ones ^1-10

and fulfilling the needs of sentient beings. ^1-11

Re-absorbing back, it transforms into the Bhagavati, the Queen of Great Knowledge Mantras, the White Parasol Crown-Protuberance of all Tathagatas, Unconquerable and Reverting All Harm. Her body color is brilliant white like the autumn moon. ^1-12

She has one face and two hands. Sitting in the vajra cross-legged posture, she completely tramples all demons and misguiding forces. ^1-13

She has three eyes, her fangs are slightly bared, and her expression is both heroic and alluring. ^1-14

Her right hand is extended over her knee in the gesture of granting refuge (varada/abhaya). ^1-15

Her left holds at her heart the golden handle of a white parasol topped with a blazing wish-fulfilling jewel. ^1-16

Her dark hair is tied in a topknot above her head, ^1-17

with the remainder flowing loosely downward. ^1-18

She is adorned with diverse jewel ornaments ^1-19

and wears heavenly garments of upper cloth and skirt. ^1-20

Her body, speech, and mind possess the unhindered power of wisdom-vajra. ^1-21

Empowered directly by all Tathagatas, ^1-22

served by all Bodhisattvas, venerated ^1-23

and praised by all Sravakas and Pratyekabuddhas, ^1-24

all World-Protectors bow at her feet and execute her commands. ^1-25

She possesses the unyielding, blazing mark— ^1-26

She is the Great Wide Vajra Goddess who holds authority over the mandala of the three realms. ^1-27

She is of a nature non-dual between the Commitment Being (Samayasattva) and the Wisdom Being (Jnanasattva), sealed by Vairochana. ^1-28

At her heart, upon a lotus and moon, stands a radiant white syllable OṀ, surrounded by the mantra rosary. ^1-29

From it, light rays stream into the ten directions. From their tips, a mountain of terrifying weapons and divine implements erupts, ^1-30

driving away all harmful spirits, obstacles, and misguiding forces beyond a perimeter of twelve yojanas. ^1-31

The earth, sky, and intermediate directions are bound by the mantra and its light rays. ^1-32

A vajra ground, fence, net, ^1-33

tent, and canopy are formed, ^1-34

surrounded on the outside by a raging wall of fire—a secure protective wheel. ^1-35

The ones to be protected enter beneath the crest of the Guru White Parasol’s canopy, ^1-36

completely sheltered by her hand gesture of granting refuge. ^1-37

<small>Conceiving this visualization, recite the extensive dharani, or short version:</small> ^1-38

OM SITATAPATRE APARAJITE SARVA GRAHANA TRASAYA TRASAYA HANA HANA HUNG HUNG HUNG HUNG PHAT PHAT SVAHA. ^1-39

<small>Zlog-’gyur The Reversion Ritual At the end of recitation, perform the reversing ritual:</small> ^1-40

From all the Tathāgatas, ^1-41

You arose from their uṣṇīṣas as the Lady White Umbrella. ^1-42

You are the Invincible Lady who turns back hostile forces. ^1-43

Together with the hosts of mudra deities and the guardians of the world, ^1-44

And all those endowed with fierce power and miraculous ability, ^1-45

Advance against the harmful spirits and obstructing forces, ^1-46

And perform the fierce enlightened activity of repelling and reversing them. ^1-47

Turn back all harmful schemes and adverse calculations of worldly existence. ^1-48

Turn back all armies and hosts of gods and spirits. ^1-49

Turn back all unfavorable divinations and astrological calculations. ^1-50

Turn back all bad dreams and evil omens. ^1-51

Turn back all unfavorable signs. ^1-52

Turn back all harmful intentions and harmful actions. ^1-53

Turn back all sorcery, curses, and harmful ritual attacks involving ritual daggers. ^1-54

Turn back all fierce magical assaults. ^1-55

Turn back the various harmful zor rites, spirit attacks, and mustard-seed zor rites. ^1-56

Turn back harm caused by earth lords and nāgas. ^1-57

Turn back the life-stealing harm of theurang and elemental spirits. ^1-58

Turn back all the deadly forces of Yama. ^1-59

Turn back all the deadly forces of Yama. ^1-60

Turn back all harmful acts of hostile non-Buddhist practitioners. ^1-61

Turn back madness and possession caused by gyalpo and sen spirits. ^1-62

Turn back harm caused by planetary spirits and celestial forces. ^1-63

Turn back all the tsho-zor rites of Zhang Zhung. ^1-64

Turn back all the ox-zor rites of Sumpa. ^1-65

Turn back all the horse-zor rites of the Bönpos. ^1-66

Turn back all harmful mantra-zor rites. ^1-67

Turn back all harmful arrow-zor rites sent by people. ^1-68

Turn back all four executioners of Māra. ^1-70

Turn back the twenty-one thousand classes of harmful spirits. ^1-75

Turn back the eighty thousand classes of obstructing forces. ^1-76

Turn back the three hundred and sixty kinds of primordial fright and disturbance. ^1-77

Turn back the fifteen great harmful spirits that afflict children. ^1-78

Turn back unfavorable periods connected with years, months, days, and astrological cycles. ^1-79

Turn back the danger of untimely death. ^1-80

Turn back every unfavorable and opposing circumstance. ^1-81

BHAGAVATE SARVA TATHAGATOCCHISHA SITATAPATRE SAPARIVARA AGHAM. PATYAM. ^1-82

PUPPE. DHUPPE. ALOKE. GANDHE. NIVIDYA. SHABDA PRATICCHAYE SVAHA. ^1-83

<small>Praise with Indian classical texts, the praises composed by Sangye Onpo, or in short:</small> ^1-84

Through the mandala of the Great Wide Vajra,
You protect living beings of the three realms from all torments,
Bestowing the fine, cool shade of benefit and happiness—
Noble Goddess White Parasol, confer supreme dharani blessings! ^1-85

<small>Pray thus. The wisdom goddess returns to her natural abode; the commitment goddess dissolves into one’s own heart; rest in luminous clarity. Know the torma offerings through the standard ritual manuals.</small> ^1-86
