# This is the Daily Practice of the Guru Yoga of the Ultimate Permanent Vajra, the Blue Akshobhya, called Clearing the Darkness of Suffering ^22-0

Homage to the Guru! ^22-1

<small>This is the Guru Yoga of Venerable Kirtishri, called Clearing the Darkness of Suffering.</small> ^22-2

<small>First, going for refuge:</small> ^22-3

Namo! The real embodiment of the Buddha together with his offspring without exception,
In the venerable and glorious sublime guru,
Myself and others, all living beings without exception,
Take refuge until we attain enlightenment (bodhiḥ)! ^22-4

<small>Generating Bodhicitta:</small> ^22-5

The mother sentient beings limitless as space,
May they have happiness and free from all sufferings,
And to swiftly attain the complete enlightenment,
I will practice the profound Guruyoga. ^22-6

<small>Thus, generating the Mind of Awakening,</small> ^22-7

OM SVA BHA WA <small>Purify with this.</small> ^22-8

From within emptiness, the dwelling places –
Akaniṣṭha, the realm of blazing mountain,
Completed the maṇḍala without exception,
In the center of the sky is the lotus, sun, and the moon (disc), ^22-9

On the cushion of the four demons (māraḥ) unite,
One’s own mind completely becomes Hūṃ syllable,
A delightful innately born body,
Clear, unshared, and complete, ^22-10

In the sphere of five-colored rainbow lights at the crown,
On the lion throne with lotus and moon seat,
The supreme embodiment of the victors of the three times,
The true embodiment of jewels of the times and directions, ^22-11

The delightful guru Heruka,
Sapphire color (indranīlaḥ) in complexion,
Pure and clear, the two hands are the symbol of skillful means and wisdom,
Holding varja and bell, ^22-12

Adorned by the ornaments of bones and so on,
A peaceful smiling with the major and minor marks blaze in glory,
Please think of your devoted son with compassion!
The powerful one who transfers the blessings, ^22-13

The protector of beings meaningful whoever is encountered,
The great universal glory of precious ones,
Vajra Jewel in the four directions,
Together with Padma Karma ḍākinī, ^22-14

Radiance of white, yellow, red, and green,
Holding a curved-knif, kapāla, and khaṭvaṅga,
The assembly of Kagyu masters in the direction above,
The meditation deity of the tantras in the middle, ^22-15

Vajra Dharma Protectors in the lower part,
Generate that ocean of objects of refuge are surrounded,
Light radiates from the three places,
The gurus and meditation deities of the Kagyu tradition, ^22-16

The hosts of retinues of ḍākinīs and dharmapāḷas,
Together with the sound of bell, bangles, and cymbals,
Invite them in an instant. <small>Think in this way.</small> ^22-17

E A RA LI PHEM. ^22-18

VAJRA SA MA DZA <small>Invoke with this.</small> ^22-19

JAH HUNG BAM HO <small>dissolving into nonduality,</small> ^22-20

NA MO NA MA HUNG <small>Prostrate with this.</small> ^22-21

OM A HUNG. ^22-22

The offerings of sense pleasures, and so on,
The outer, inner, secret, and suchness,
The great mudrā of offerings,
I offer to you, venerable guru! ^22-23

The four continents, sub-continents, and the sun, the moon, and so on,
The assemblage wealth of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa,
Having taken by the mind without exception,
Through unstinting mind,
I offer to the Venerble (Jetsun) together with his retinues! ^22-24

OM SHRI MAN GURU CHARANA SAPARIVARA ARGHAM <small>[onward]</small> ^22-25

SHABDA RUPA GHANDHE RASA SPARSHE DHARMA DHATU ATMAKO HAM. ^22-26

<small>Thus, make offering.</small> ^22-27

Owing to his kindness, the very great bliss,
Whatever arises in an instant,
Guru with jewel-like body,
I bow at the lotus of one who possesses vajra! ^22-28

Liberating beings from the ocean of cyclic existence,
Leading them to the state of omniscience,
The supreme sovereign lord of all families,
I pay homage to the glorious guru! ^22-29

For welfare of beings in this degenerated time,
Marvelous infusion of compassion,
All the offspring of the victors, please think of us!
To you, the Venerable One, I praise with devotion! ^22-30

Ourselves and all living beings without exception,
Without remaining in the end of existence and nirvāṇa,
May we attain the supreme state of you –
the Venerable master! ^22-31

<small>Praise with this, then, applying mindfulness,</small> ^22-32

OM AH NAMO RATNA GURU KIRTI SHRI RASMI BHADRAYA HUNG. ^22-33

<small>Recite this as much as you can. In the end, with a strong and unflagging devotion in guru, with a single-pointed mind merged with the faith of total reliance, recite these sacred words of the past Kagyu forefathers:</small> ^22-34

My mothers, all sentient beings who fill space, pray to the Guru, Precious Buddha! ^22-35

My mothers, all sentient beings who fill space, pray to the Guru, the all-pervading Dharmakaya! ^22-36

My mothers, all sentient beings who fill space, pray to the Guru, the Great Bliss Sambhogakaya! ^22-37

My mothers, all sentient beings who fill space, pray to the Guru, the compassionate Nirmanakaya! ^22-38

Kye! Kye! Venerable guru,
The universal form of victors of the three times,
The guide of living beings pervading the sky,
In you, the Venerable guru,
I take refuge until I attain enlightenment! ^22-39

I and all living beings without exception,
Wandering in this state of existence (saṃsāraḥ),
The one who disregards the cause and effect
Is a savage like me, please look upon me, ^22-40

The venerable guru, who is the teacher,
The essential nature embodying the Three Jewels,
The essence of family and hundreds of maṇḍalas,
The Venerable Guru, please think of me! ^22-41

At the feet of incomparable glorious renown,
The protector, wherever you are,
We are your lineage holders,
Destroy the thoughts of the eight worldly concerns! ^22-42

Liberate the enemy of self-centeredness!
Bestow the light of wisdom!
Lead us to the ground (bhūmiḥ) of omniscience! ^22-43

<small>With this, make supplications until you have developed a genuine devotion unpredictable of reckoning. Having made this supplication, imagine that through extremely delighted mode, bestowing the four empowerment in a single sitting.</small> ^22-44

Through this supplication,
The retinues turned into mass of light,
And dissolves into the venerable master (guru),
From the seed of guru’s three points, ^22-45

Radiating white, red, and blue light rays,
Having dissolved into my three points,
Which purifies the four obscurations and bestows the four empowerments,
And actualize the four kāyas! ^22-46

Finally, with a strong and fervent devotion,
The Venerable Sage appears as mass of light
Which enters the crown and merge at the heart center,
Purifying the co-emergent obscurations,
Complete perfection of the four actual empowerments, ^22-47

The three secrets of the Venerable master, and
Our own body, speech, and mind,
Are nondual, beyond conceptual thoughts, and the great bliss,
Accomplished as the great seal (mahāmudrā) of all that appears and exists! ^22-48

<small>With this, sustain the uncontrived innate awareness. At the end, imagine the merits gathered from this offer to all mother sentient beings.</small> ^22-49

Through the virtue of this,
Just as the Youth Excellent Wealth (Kumāra-Sudhana),
Like the ocean of good qualities,
May this please the sublime guru! ^22-50

<small>With this, recite dedication, aspiration, and auspicious verses, and so on, whatever is appropriate. I, Karma Maitrikara holding the name Jetrul, follower of Jetsun Kīrtiśrī, had the auspicious signs and indications to write this Guruyoga when I was on a strict retreat meditation at LhalungDrukri (Divine Valley of Dragon Mountain) in the year of Wood-Monkey, however, I did not put a great effort to write. Again, at the request made by several interested and zealous people and according to the words of Kīrtiśr, I wrote this on my festival, so I think there is a slight bit of blessing. Sarva-Maṅgalam! Virtue! Virtue! Virtue!</small> ^22-51
