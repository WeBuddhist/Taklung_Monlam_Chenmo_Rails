# Condensed Birth Stories of Thangpa ^0

## Condensed Birth Stories of Thangpa ^15-0

The victorious Vajradhara in the Aknishtha realm,
Indrabhuti in the practice place,
The glorious Tashi Pel in this land of snow,
The well-known Thangpa Chenpo, to you I pay homage! ^15-1

The lord Akshobhya in the realm free from dust (viraja),
The lord Avalokiteshvara in Potala,
The incomparable Tashi Pel in this land of snow,
The well-known Thangpa Chenpo, to you I pay homage! ^15-2

Rasa Trulnang, the King of Emanation,
At U, the supreme Arya Chayulwa in the upper part,
The incomparable Tashi Pel in this plain of Talkung,
The well-known Thangpa Chenpo, to you I pay homage! ^15-3

<small>This Supplication of Condensed Birth stories of Taklungpa Chenpo is written by Bhikshu Namgyal Rinchen Pal Zangpo.</small> ^15-4
