# Herein is contained “The Torma Dedication, Source of All Siddhis.” ^0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-0]]

## Herein is contained “The Torma Dedication, Source of All Siddhis.” ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-1]]

OṂ! Within the outer, inner, secret, and suchness offering vessel,
All conditioned and unconditioned things become the substances of the torma.
Through the fire and wind of method and wisdom—the spontaneously arisen inner heat—
They are purified, dissolved, and set ablaze, becoming the nectar of primordial wisdom. ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-2]]

OṂĀḤ HŪṂ HA HO HRĪḤ — <small>three times.</small> ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-3]]

I myself am primordially and completely perfected as Cakrasaṃvara.
From the seed syllable at the heart of the co-emergent Father and Mother,
The root and lineage gurus, the hosts of yidam deities,
Ḍākinīs, Dharma protectors and their retinues—the guests of the torma— ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-4]]

Are invited into the sky before me.
From their tongues, tubes of light extend into the torma.
By partaking of it, may they experience an array of the supreme flavors of everything delightful. ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-5]]

OM AH HUNG GURU DHI VA DAKKINI IDAM BALINTA KHA KHA KHAHI KHAHI. ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-6]]

HŪṂ! All-pervading Lord Vajradhara, mighty holder of the vajra,
Nāgārjuna, Āryadeva, and Aśvaghoṣa,
Glorious Dīpaṃkara and the great Vajrāsana,
Abhaya, Tsami, and the great glorious Ga,
Lama Aseng and Drögön Dorje Gyal,
And Thangpa together with his spiritual heirs—to you all I make offerings. ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-7]]

HŪṂ! Great Vajradhara, Tilopa and Nāropa,
Marpa, Milarepa and Lord Gampopa,
Drögön Chöje Taklung Thangpa,
Kuyal, Yarbjön, and the venerable Tashi Lama, ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-8]]

Sangye Palzang and the venerable Ratna Guru,
Ratnākara and Künpang Pal Tsekpa,
Jangchub Gyatso and the one named Ngagi Wangpo,
Namgyal Drakpa and Namgyal Tashi Je, ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-9]]

Kunga Tashi and Ngawang NamparGyal,
Tashi Paldrub and DrakpaLekdrub Je,
Jampal Gyatso and Phagpa Tashi Wang,
Tenzin Namgyal, supreme siddha Phülden, ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-10]]

Maṅgala Kīrti and JetsünChö Gocha,
Rinchen Lekdrub and the one named ChöyingLhündrub,
Chökyi Gyaltsen and the venerable DrakpaKünsal,
The root guru embodying all sources of refuge, and all the others— ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-11]]

To the Kagyu gurus, the root of blessings,
I offer this torma of samaya substance and nectar.
Please cause a continuous rain of supreme blessings to descend. ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-12]]

HŪṂ! The supreme yidam, Cakrasaṃvara Father and Mother,
Together with all the hosts of deities of the five chakras without exception;
Hevajra, the Four Seats, Mahāmāyā, and the Skull deity;
Vajrāmṛta, Hūṃkāra, and Kālacakra; ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-13]]

Glorious Guhyasamāja and the Red and Black Yamāntakas;
The oceans of peaceful and wrathful victorious deities of the New and Ancient traditions—
To you I offer this torma of samaya substance and nectar.
Please cause a continuous rain of supreme siddhis to descend. ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-14]]

HŪṂ! The power of the wrathful enlightened activity of all the Victorious Ones,
Four-Armed Black Mahākāla, who subdues the unruly;
The three Mothers of body, speech and mind, and the Four Lords of Activity;
The sixty figures headed by the six groups of eight ḍākinīs; ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-15]]

The twenty-one male demons and the twenty-eight powerful lords;
The thirty haughty spirits and the seventy-two glorious protectors;
The hundred vetālas and the thousand Great Black Ones;
The hundred thousand flesh-eaters and the ten million mamos; ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-16]]

Together with the ocean-like hosts of protectors of the teachings— To you I offer this torma of samaya substance and nectar.
May the precious teachings flourish throughout the ten directions.
Completely vanquish and repel all adverse conditions,
Obstacles, and opposing forces that hinder practitioners.
Perform this enlightened activity without exception. ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-17]]

HŪṂ! Six-Armed Mahākāla, swift in bestowing blessings;
Glorious Devī, sovereign of the desire realm;
Kṣetrapāla and Jinamitra;
Trakkirāja, Dü Gön, and great Trakshé; ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-18]]

The ten guardians of the directions and the seventy-five Tsang protectors;
Together with the ocean-like retinue of oath-bound protectors—
I offer offerings and tormas, filling the whole expanse of space.
Cause the teachings of the sovereign masters Marpa and Mila to flourish,
And accomplish every aim desired by yogic practitioners. ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-19]]

HŪṂ! Lekden Nakpo, the Protector, father of a hundred thousand protectors
Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Wangmo and Drakmo;
Nakmo, Genjé, Zhönnu and Phagmo—
The eight yoginīs who guard the various directions; ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-20]]

Dütsen, Düyug, Shinyug and Shintsanma;
Together with the ocean-like retinues of oath-bound protectors—
I offer you this torma of samaya substance and nectar.
Repel hostile and malicious armies from the borderlands. ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-21]]

As long as this great Dharma wheel remains beneath the sky,
Guard and protect it without negligence,
And perform enlightened activity that increases the glory of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa. ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-22]]

HŪṂ! Śrī Gur Mahākāla, glorious command-protector of the tantras;
Vikāla, mighty lord of inexhaustible power and strength;
Together with the ocean of messengers who obey your commands—
I offer this torma of samaya substance and nectar. ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-23]]

May the precious teachings flourish throughout the ten directions.
Completely vanquish and repel all adverse conditions,
Obstacles, and opposing forces to Dharma practice. Perform this enlightened activity without exception. ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-24]]

HŪṂ! Four-Faced Glorious Mahākāla, protector of the sacred lands;
The four great Mothers, the twenty-one executioners, and the others;
Together with the eight classes of gods and spirits and all their retinues—
I offer this torma of samaya substance and nectar. ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-25]]

In accordance with the vajra command of the primordial glorious Lord,
Increase the teachings and all that is excellent for the teachings in this place.
For as long as the enlightened activity of the assembled vidyādharas and liberated ones remains,
Cause it continually to flourish, increase, and endure. ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-26]]

BHYO! Düsolma, sovereign lady of the vajra expanse;
The three sisters who obey the command, and the wisdom protectresses;
The hosts of a hundred thousand classes of mamos, some arisen from wisdom and others from karma;
Protectors and guardians of the teachings together with their retinues— ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-27]]

I offer you this torma of samaya substance and nectar.
May the precious teachings flourish throughout the ten directions.
Completely vanquish and repel all adverse conditions, Obstacles,
and opposing forces to Dharma practice. Perform this enlightened activity without exception. ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-28]]

HŪṂ! Powerful emanated attendants who are actual manifestations of the Great Glorious One,
Who through wrathful enlightened activity destroy the enemies of the teachings;
Trakshé Father and Mother, together with the Mönpa and armies of māras—
I offer you this torma of samaya substance and nectar. ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-29]]

May the precious teachings flourish throughout the ten directions.
Completely vanquish and repel all adverse conditions, Obstacles,
and opposing forces to Dharma practice. Perform this enlightened activity without exception.
VAI! You who emanate many jewels, lord of the treasury of space, ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-30]]

Great and virtuous yakṣa of mighty strength who brings relief,
Holy commander and king, Vaiśravaṇa,
Together with the Eight Lords of Horses and an ocean of attendants—
I offer you this torma of samaya substance and nectar. ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-31]]

Through the power of samaya, joyfully accept it.
Free us from miserliness, karmic obscurations, and all their causes and results.
With every form of wealth that accords with the Dharma,
Satisfy myself and all beings as limitless as space. ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-32]]

HRĪḤ! Invincible lord of the yakṣas,
Together with your queen, sons, ministers and subjects,
Surrounded by hundreds of billions of divisions of yakṣas—
I offer you this torma of samaya substance and nectar. ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-33]]

Through the power of samaya, joyfully accept it.
Free us from miserliness, karmic obscurations, and all their causes and results.
With every form of wealth that accords with the Dharma,
Satisfy myself and all beings as limitless as space. ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-34]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-35]]

OṂĀḤ KĀRO — <small>three times.</small> ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-36]]

JA JAḤ, IDAṂ … — <small>three times.</small> ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-37]]

KYAI! The seventy-five glorious Tsang protectors,
The Twelve Tenma goddesses and the twenty-one upāsakas,
The divine medicine goddess Tashi Tseyi Wangchuk and her companions,
Especially the upāsaka Dorje Künkyob, ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-38]]

The local lord and commander Yi Gyal Rinchen, and the others—
To the local gods and territorial guardians who protect the virtuous side,
I offer this torma of samaya substance and nectar.
Pacify invading armies and epidemics, and bring peace to the world. ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-39]]

Clear away the obstacles of practitioners and establish favorable conditions.
Cause everything wished for in the mind to be accomplished in an instant. ^1-39

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-40]]

[Then recite the section beginning with “Glorious Mikyö…” <small>and so forth.</small>] ^1-40

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-41]]

NAMO MANGA SHRIYE HUNG. ^1-41

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-42]]

<small>Here, a concise supplication and offering is to be made to the two great Dharma protectors of our own tradition. The torma should be prepared according to the customary tradition.</small> ^1-42

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-43]]

From the heart of oneself clearly visualized as the deity, rays of light radiate forth. <small>Imagine that</small> the great protectors of the teachings, together with their retinues, are invited. ^1-43

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-44]]

KYAI! Great divine upāsaka endowed with fierce power,
Commander of the māra armies, mighty Yi Chen Rabnge Tsal,
Together with your sons, consorts, ministers, subjects and retinues—
Remember your former pledge and solemn oath,
And please come to this supreme and sacred place. ^1-44

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-45]]

OM VAJRA SAMAYA JA JAH HUNG BAM HO. ^1-45

SAMAYA TISHTHA LHAN. ^1-46

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-47]]

OM VAJRA SAMAYA HANA HANA HUNG PHAT. ^1-47

VAJRA ARGHAM PĀDYAM <small>…through to:</small> ^1-48

MAHA PANCHA KHAHI. MAHA RAKTA KHAHI. ^1-50

CHANDRA PRAMANI DHARA DHARA SAMAYA JA JAH SAPARIVARA EDAM <small>etc.</small> ^1-51

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-52]]

DHASHAGHRIVA DUJAH SAPARIVARA EDAM <small>etc.</small> ^1-52

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-53]]

Great divine upāsaka, the fierce and powerful tsen spirit of Drak, Commander Yi Gyal Rinchen, together with your entire retinue: Please accept this vast sacrificial torma offering. ^1-53

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-54]]

Protect the teachings of the Buddha. ^1-54

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-55]]

Exalt the honor and dignity of the Three Jewels. ^1-55

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-56]]

Protect the Saṅgha and the Dharma kingdom. ^1-56

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-57]]

Remove the decline and misfortunes of the world. ^1-57

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-58]]

Increase the benefit and happiness of sentient beings. ^1-58

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-59]]

Be companions and helpers to yogic practitioners. ^1-59

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-60]]

Accomplish the enlightened activities of mantra holders. ^1-60

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-61]]

Subdue hostile enemies. ^1-61

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-62]]

Overcome harmful obstructing forces. ^1-62

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-63]]

In particular, pacify all outer, inner, and secret adverse conditions of everyone gathered here within this sacred samaya. ^1-63

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-64]]

Cause all favorable conditions and every excellence we aspire to To increase and flourish. ^1-64

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-65]]

Perform the enlightened activity that swiftly accomplishes, without exception, All supreme and ordinary siddhis. ^1-65

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-66]]

KYAI! Warm nourishment that restores and nurtures life,
Powerful means for subduing enemies and obstructing forces;
Pure food piled as high as Mount Meru,
A beautiful and magnificent silken spear-banner,
And pure milk-water together with medicinal substances— ^1-66

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-67]]

These I offer to the divine tsen protector and his retinue.
Accept them, enjoy them, and be pleased.
In order to deprive the enemies and obstructing forces Who hate the teachings of their power,
strength and magical abilities, ^1-67

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-68]]

O mighty Tsen King, ride forth to battle!
Rouse the armies of the vast hosts of tsen spirits!
Crush the power of enemies and obstructing forces!
Increase the teachings of the virtuous side!
Maintain the proper order of karmic good and evil! ^1-68

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-69]]

This beautiful divine food offering
Is a thanksgiving for your excellent deeds in the past.
Now again, accept this offering as I entrust you with further activity.
Cause myself and others to flourish and prosper,
And accomplish the enlightened activities entrusted to you. ^1-69

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-70]]

Omniscient Lord of the Victorious Ones, sovereign possessor of the ten powers.
Taklungpa, the peerless guide, without equal.
The vajra command of the venerable Sangye Önpo Was entrusted as an unsurpassable samaya that must never be transgressed.
You, powerful and swift upāsaka, endowed with fierce strength, ^1-70

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-71]]

Command-protector of the teachings, divine guardian
of the virtuous side,
Yi Gyal Rinchen—to you at all times
I make abundant offerings of every excellent substance. ^1-71

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-72]]

When I supplicate you closely with words of praise and glorification,
Cause all the aims and wishes for which I, the yogin,
Pray To be accomplished exactly as desired, right now.
In the Snow Land, master of the teachings of scripture and realization, ^1-72

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-73]]

Taklungpa, holder of the chariot tradition of teaching and practice—
The precious victory banner of his spiritual and temporal teachings
Pervades the entire expanse of the sky throughout the three realms.
Powerful one, accomplish all these supreme enlightened activities! ^1-73

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-74]]

You possess great and swift miraculous power,
Sending forth various emanations in every single instant.
You crush the armies of hostile, unruly forces that lead beings astray,
And cause the teachings of the Victorious One to shine like daylight. ^1-74

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-75]]

At such times, you assume a terrifying appearance Of fierce and uncontrollable wrath,
A wrathful form upon which no one can bear to gaze,
Appearing in the manner of a savage and ferocious executioner.
You have mastery over the activities of slaying and liberating.
I praise you, powerful and fierce executor of enlightened activity! ^1-75

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-76]]

Through pacifying activity, cause the teachings to flourish.
Through magnetizing activity, gather the three realms under your influence.
Through enriching activity, increase wealth, prosperity and power.
Through wrathful activity, pulverize enemies and obstructing forces into dust. ^1-76

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-77]]

Throughout all these regions, countries, and lands,
May auspiciousness, happiness, goodness and perfect abundance prevail! ^1-77

![[1-SOURCES/Text/23. གཏོར་བསྔོས་དངོས་གྲུབ་ཀུན་འབྱུང་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-78]]

<small>Thus, offer the torma in a clean place. This too, being needed for practical use, was compiled and arranged, and written by one bearing the name Maitrī. ŚUBHAM — May there be goodness and auspiciousness!</small> ^1-78
