# Confessing the Downfalls of the Bodhisattava ^24-0

All sentient beings continually go for refuge to the Lama, ^24-1

go for refuge to the Buddha, ^24-2

go for refuge to the Dharma, ^24-3

go for refuge to the Sangha. ^24-4

I bow down to the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror, Thus-Gone, Foe-Destroyer, the Perfect Buddha, Sage of the Shakyas [Bhagavan Tathagata Arhat Samyak Sambuddha Shakyamuni]! ^24-5

I bow down to Supremely Destroying by Vajra Heart [Vajrapramardi]! ^24-6

I bow down to Precious Light Rays [Ratnacisha]! ^24-7

I bow down to King of Naga Lords [Nagesvararaja]! ^24-8

I bow down to Class of Heroes [Virasena]! ^24-9

I bow down to Glorious Delight [Viranandin]! ^24-10

I bow down to Precious Fire [Ratnagni]! ^24-11

I bow down to Precious Moonlight [Ratnachandraprabha]! ^24-12

I bow down to Meaningful to See [Amoghadarshin]! ^24-13

I bow down to Precious Moon [Ratnachandra]! ^24-14

I bow down to Stainless [Nirmala]! ^24-15

I bow down to Glorious Gift [Shridatta]! ^24-16

I bow down to Purity [Brahma]! ^24-17

I bow down to Pure Gift [Brahmadatta]! ^24-18

I bow down to Water Deity [Varuna]! ^24-19

I bow down to God of Water Deities [Varunadeva]! ^24-20

I bow down to Glorious Good [Bhadrashri]! ^24-21

I bow down to Glorious Sandalwood [Chandanashri]! ^24-22

I bow down to Limitless Brilliance [Anantaujashri]! ^24-23

I bow down to Glorious Light [Prabhashri]! ^24-24

I bow down to Glorious Sorrowlessness [Ashokashri]! ^24-25

I bow down to Unborn Son [Narayana]! ^24-26

I bow down to Glorious Flower [Kusumashri]! ^24-27

I bow down to Thus-gone Pure Light Rays Complete Enjoyment Total Omniscience [Brahmajyotirvikriditabhija Tathagata]! ^24-28

I bow down to Thus-gone Lotus Light Rays Complete Enjoyment Total Omniscience [Padmajyotirvikriditabhija Tathagata]! ^24-29

I bow down to Glorious Wealth [Dhanashri]! ^24-30

I bow down to Glorious Mindfulness [Smritishri]! ^24-31

I bow down to Extremely Famous Glorious Signs [Suparikirtitanamagheyashri]! ^24-32

I bow down to King of the Victory Banner of Indra’s Realization [Indraketudhvajaraja]! ^24-33

I bow down to Glorious Extremely Complete Subjugation [Suvikrantashri]! ^24-34

I bow down to Extremely Completely Victorious in Battle [Vijitasamgrama]! ^24-35

I bow down to Departed by Complete Subjugation [Vikrantagamin]! ^24-36

I bow down to Glorious Array of All Appearances [Samantavabhasavyuhashri]! ^24-37

I bow down to Precious Lotus Complete Subjugation [Ratnapadmavikramin]! ^24-38

I bow down to Thus-gone Foe-destroyer Perfectly Perfected Buddha Precious Lotus Supremely Abiding Powerful King of Mountains [Tathagata Arhat Samyak sambuddhaRatnapadmaSupratishthita-shailendraraja]! ^24-39

These and others, as many Thus-gone Foe-Destroyer Perfect Buddha Accomplished Transcendent Conquerors as there are abiding in the ten direction realms of this perishable universe-all you Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror Buddhas, please think of me! ^24-40

During this life of mine, and through births without beginning or end, in all my births, circling in Samsara, I have committed evil actions, and ^24-41

instigated their doing, and ^24-42

rejoiced in their being done. ^24-43

[I have stolen] offerings from stupas, ^24-44

offerings from the [local] Sangha, ^24-45

and robbed offerings from the Sangha in the ten directions. ^24-46

I have instigated others to steal, andI have rejoiced in the robbery of others. ^24-47

I have committed the Five Limitless Actions, and ^24-48

I have instigated others to commit them, and I have rejoiced in their being done. ^24-49

I have fully given rise to and remained on the path of the ten non-virtuous actions, and ^24-50

Instigated others to enter it and rejoiced at [their] entering it. ^24-51

Whatever [actions I have done] obscured by the shadow of karma, causing myself and all sentient beings to be born in the hells, ^24-52

causing birth in the animal realm, ^24-53

Causing birth in the world of hungry spirits, ^24-54

birth in a border [non-Dharmic] country, ^24-55

birth among barbarians, ^24-56

birth as one of the long-lived gods, ^24-57

birth as one with incomplete faculties, ^24-58

[or causing one] to become a holder of perverted views, ^24-59

Whatever obscurations of karma that displease the Buddhas, [in front of] the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror Buddhas, who are Pristine Awareness, ^24-60

who see, who witness, ^24-61

who measure fully, who understand by observation, ^24-62

in the presence of them all, I openly admit and confess, ^24-63

without concealing, without hiding, ^24-64

from this time forward, I promise never to commit them again. ^24-65

Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror Buddhas, all of you please think of me! ^24-66

During this life of mine, and through births without beginning or end, in all my other births, circling in Samsara, whatever roots of virtue I have accumulated through offering even a handful of food to one born as an animal, and ^24-67

whatever roots of virtue I have accumulated through guarding ethical discipline, and ^24-68

whatever roots of virtue I have accumulated through abiding in pure conduct, and ^24-69

whatever roots of virtue I have accumulated through completely ripening sentient beings, and ^24-70

whatever roots of virtue I have accumulated through giving rise to the Mind of Supreme Enlightenment, and ^24-71

whatever virtuous roots of unsurpassable Pristine Awareness there may be, gathering all of these together into one, [by dedicating it] to what is unsurpassable, and ^24-72

to what is highest, and to what is higher than highest, ^24-73

to what is unsurpassably unsurpassable, I completely dedicate it to unsurpassable, perfectly perfected Enlightenment! ^24-74

Just as the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror Buddhas of the past completely dedicated, and ^24-75

just as the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror Buddhas who have not yet come will completely dedicate, and ^24-76

Just as the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror Buddhas who have now arisen are now completely dedicating, just in that way, I also completely dedicate this merit! ^24-77

I individually confess all evil actions! ^24-78

I rejoice in all merits! ^24-79

Exhorting all the Buddhas, I supplicate them! ^24-80

May unsurpassable, supreme, sacred Original Awareness suddenly arise in me! ^24-81

Whatever Victorious Ones, supreme among humans, who are now abiding, and ^24-82

those who have passed away, and likewise those who have not yet come, ^24-83

in your vast ocean of praiseworthy enlightened qualities, ^24-84

joining my hands, I seek refuge! ^24-85

<small>This completes the Sutra of the Three Aggregates.</small> ^24-86

The three [negative] actions of body,
The four of speech, and
Whichever of the three of mind,
[I] confess the Ten Non-Virtues individually. ^24-87

From beginning-lessness down to the present,
The Ten Non-virtues and the Five Limitless Acts,
[committed] under the power of mind’s afflictions,
I confess all evil actions! ^24-88

By the power of desire-attachment, hatred, and ignorance,
with body and speech and also likewise with mind,
Whatever evil actions I have committed,
I confess all of them individually! ^24-89

All my evil deeds, the Five Limitless Actions,
Whatever I have committed under the power of ignorance,
by reciting this Confession of Downfalls,
May they all, without exception, be quickly and completely purified! ^24-90
