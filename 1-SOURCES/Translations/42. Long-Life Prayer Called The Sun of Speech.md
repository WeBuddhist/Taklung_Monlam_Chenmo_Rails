# Long-Life Prayer Called “The Sun of Speech” ^0

## Long-Life Prayer Called “The Sun of Speech” ^42-0

O Sun of Speech, supreme guide and crown of the Śākyas;
the seventeen great scholars and accomplished masters;
Tilopa and Nāropa; Marpa, Milarepa, and Dagpo;TaklungThangpa-
and all the lineage lamas: please grant your blessings of virtue and goodness. ^42-1

Buddha Amitāyus, lord of the victorious ones, moved by compassion for beings of the five classes,
you bestow the supreme gift of an indestructible life.
Please bestow here today your great blessing for the attainment of deathlessness.
Please bestow here today your great blessing for the attainment of deathlessness. ^42-2

Through the power of your noble aspiration
to uphold the sublime treatises of the wonderful Buddha,
you have become a source of goodness for beings living in this age of five degenerations.
O Chökyi Wangchuk, may your lotus feet remain firm. ^42-3

Without favouring only one of hearing, reflection, and meditation,
you live the exemplary life of one who is learned, disciplined, and good.
You preserve and spread the Buddha’s teachings, both in general and in your own tradition.
O peerless great holder of the teachings, may you live long. ^42-4

By the pure nature of dependent arising
and the power of the truth of the precious Three Jewels,
may every aim of this prayer
be fulfilled easily and without obstacles. ^42-5

<small>TaklungMatrul Rinpoche requested that, when the hair of the young reincarnation of TaklungTsetrul Rinpoche was cut in the customary ceremony, the child also be given a name and a long-life prayer. In response, the Dalai Lama gave the child the name Tenzin Chökyi Wangchuk and composed this prayer. He wrote it on the twelfth day of the fourth month of the Fire Bird year of the seventeenth Tibetan cycle, corresponding to June 6, 2017. May it be virtuous.</small> ^42-6
