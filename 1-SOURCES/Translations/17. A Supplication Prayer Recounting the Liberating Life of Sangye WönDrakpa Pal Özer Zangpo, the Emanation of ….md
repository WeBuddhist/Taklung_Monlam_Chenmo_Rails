# A Supplication Prayer Recounting the Liberating Life of Sangye WönDrakpa Pal Özer Zangpo, the Emanation of the Supreme Venerable Master ^0

## A Supplication Prayer Recounting the Liberating Life of Sangye WönDrakpa Pal Özer Zangpo, the Emanation of the Supreme Venerable Master ^17-0

Homage to the precious Guru Hevajra! ^17-1

O incomparable protector, you descended from the expanse of space.
Your spiritual lineage is the unchanging lineage of Vajradhara.
The dakinis gave prophecies and urged the two, Jo and Shak.
I supplicate you, the one endowed with boundless compassion. ^17-2

Although you entered your mother’s womb, you were free from worldly attachment and aversion.
You intentionally took birth, thinking only of benefiting sentient beings.
You practiced the six daily sessions of Hevajra yoga without negligence.
I supplicate to you, who had trained in the two stages of tantric practice. ^17-3

You were born peacefully, without causing suffering to your mother.
When your umbilical cord was cut, the sound changed into the music of a lute.
You spoke twenty-one verses of the sacred Dharma.
I supplicate you, who resembled the son of King Shuddhodana, the Buddha. ^17-4

Surrounded by rainbow light, you rose into the sky and remained there.
You recited the Añja mantra and the Sanskrit vowels and consonants.
You said to your mother, “Keep this extremely secret.”
I supplicate to you, who performed your activities in a concealed manner. ^17-5

When you were invited to the center of a pure and sacred place,
You received the prophecy of a hero and saw the wings of Hevajra’sgaruda.
At the age of three, the treasury of vajra songs burst forth from you.
I supplicate you, the exceptionally noble one. ^17-6

Like a miraculous child, you performed many playful activities.
You explained many kinds of letters, sounds, and systems of language.
You demonstrated various ways of making images of the victorious Buddhas.
I supplicate you, who perfected all fields of learning. ^17-7

You shaped the figure of a naga from solid rock as easily as molding butter.
Upon water, you established a firm and lasting sacred image.
You entered the unfathomable depths of the earth as swiftly as a thunderbolt.
I supplicate you, the one without equal. ^17-8

You caused downward-flowing water to rise upward like flames.
You walked steadily across steep cliffs as though they were ordinary paths.
You showed especially great compassion toward helpless children.
I supplicate you, the supreme son of the victorious Buddhas. ^17-9

A piece of wood, only a finger’s length, blazed by itself during the night.
You raised a blessed mandala into the expanse of the sky.
You transformed the sacred substance of mada into uncontaminated nectar.
I supplicate you, who performed wonders resembling magical illusions. ^17-10

In a single night, you visited the great sacred places of Uddiyana.
You received complete empowerments from the eight renowned mahasiddhas.
You were given a fresh, moist ear of victorious rice.
I supplicate you, who possessed extraordinary spiritual accomplishments. ^17-11

In the morning, you demonstrated how to guide gatherings of kings and ministers.
In the evening, you appeared as an ordained monk and turned the wheel of Dharma.
You gave nectar from your mouth that tasted like sweet sugar.
I supplicate you, who performed many activities for the benefit of sentient beings. ^17-12

You brought forth a supreme image of Hayagriva from the side of a rock.
Through the naga-tree Brahmin, you subdued a yaksha by your power.
The life-essence of NyenkharNakpo was offered directly to you.
I supplicate you, the one endowed with miraculous power and strength. ^17-13

You opened the doors of hidden treasures at several great sacred places of accomplished masters.
In the middle of a fireplace, you revealed the nine deities of Hevajra.
Through skillful use of auspicious dependent connections, you turned away harm threatening the region.
I supplicate you, whose blessings reached perfection. ^17-14

You directly inspired the sorrowful songs of the wisdom dakinis.
You explained the meanings of questions and answers in symbolic language, in accordance with the tantras.
An ocean of dakinis honored you as the ornament upon their crowns.
I supplicate you, who mastered the meaning of symbolic language. ^17-15

You appeared as the sacred regent of the Buddha, the Teacher.
The glory of your meaningful and excellent name spread widely.
Inseparable as father and son, you radiated miraculous rays of light.
I supplicate you, who possessed the excellent marks and signs of an enlightened being. ^17-16

You remained free from the faults of the two hundred and fifty monastic precepts.
You mastered bodhicitta mind-training and the practice of exchanging yourself with others.
You directly realized the profound generation and completion stages.
I supplicate you, who perfected the three sets of vows. ^17-17

From Buddhas down to shravakas and pratyekabuddhas,
You revealed many different visions to those who had the appropriate karmic connections.
You developed deep and unshakable certainty, free from dispute.
I supplicate you, whose every teaching heard was meaningful. ^17-18

You practiced Naropa’s path of skillful means, applying the essential points of channels and winds.
You brought the moving vital winds into the central channel, the dhuti.
You perceived everything that arose as the great true nature of reality.
I supplicate you, whose mind realized emptiness. ^17-19

You beheld the mandalas of the ocean of all victorious Buddhas.
You understood the realizations of all the learned and accomplished masters of India and Tibet.
You received prophecies from all the dakinis of the three places.
I supplicate you, whose activities for the benefit of beings were spontaneously accomplished. ^17-20

To those who were deeply confused, you especially demonstrated powerful yogic conduct.
You twisted the handle of a white stone as though it were woolen thread.
You caused great thunderbolts to fall like rain from a cloudless sky.
I supplicate you, who subdued wrong views. ^17-21

Upon the waters of the autumn month, you arranged a silken pathway for the dakinis.
At that time, you prepared to depart for another pure realm of emanation.
When supplications were offered, you once again looked upon your disciples with compassion.
I supplicate you, who continued to care for fortunate disciples. ^17-22

You left raised footprints upon the solid rocks at Thaden.
You spread the sacred Dharma extensively to beings without distinction of direction.
You guided the three kinds of spiritual practitioners onto the path of enlightenment.
I supplicate you, whose mind was filled with bodhicitta. ^17-23

You transformed this remote and uncivilized region into a pure Buddha-field.
You clearly manifested the arrangement of Vajrasana, the Vajra Seat.
You opened the treasure doors of all desirable enjoyments and necessities.
I supplicate you, who resembled a universal wheel- turning monarch. ^17-24

Jñānatapa escorted the sacred Dharma to your door.
Upari offered a treasury of scriptures into your hands.
You received the complete oral transmissions of all the Buddha’s teachings.
I supplicate you, whose aspiration prayers possessed great power. ^17-25

You constructed many supreme sacred supports representing enlightened body, speech, and mind.
You spread the teachings of both Sutra and Tantra extensively.
You brought the traditions of teaching and practice to complete fulfillment.
I supplicate you, the heir and representative of the victorious Buddhas. ^17-26

Through the power of miraculous abilities, you traveled throughout the three-thousandfold universe.
You entrusted the blessed lineage transmission to one who was like yourself.
You promised to appear again as an emanation to uphold the Dharma lineage.
I supplicate you, who worked for the benefit of future generations. ^17-27

When the time came for you to depart for the pure realm, you adorned your body with six ornaments.
In your hands, you held sacred substances symbolizing skillful means and wisdom.
You rose gracefully and transferred your consciousness into the great unobstructed expanse.
I supplicate you, the incomparable one. ^17-28

At that time, the earth shook in six different ways.
Many kinds of musical sounds were heard throughout the ten directions.
Hevajra yogic forms were seen dissolving into the sky.
I supplicate you, who attained the supreme great accomplishment. ^17-29

When your sacred body was placed upon the funeral fire, it was difficult to ignite.
When Punyashri offered supplications,
Fire arose from the center of your heart, and the body was consumed in an instant.
I supplicate you, who showed exceptional compassion toward me. ^17-30

Five-colored rainbows gathered in the sky like clouds.
Many different flowers fell, filling the atmosphere.
Various rays of light radiated throughout the ten directions.
I supplicate you, the one whose miraculous wonders were extraordinary. ^17-31

As sacred supports of your enlightened mind, the pañcarekha appeared,
Together with a clearly visible syllable HAM, symbolizing the unborn nature.
Countless relics, sacred remains, and small reliquary stupas appeared.
I supplicate you, whose signs of spiritual accomplishment were exceptional. ^17-32

You realized the meaning of the syllable AH, which represents the unborn nature.
You emanated from the enlightened mind of the primordial Buddha, the boundless ancestor of all Buddhas.
You were the spiritual son who carried out the enlightened activities of all Buddhas of the ten directions.
I supplicate you, whose excellent name was truly meaningful. ^17-33

In this five-hundred-year period, you were as rare as the udumbara flower.
You were supreme among those who worked for the teachings of Buddha Shakyamuni.
You were a spiritual son directly emanated from Avalokiteshvara.
I supplicate you, the emanation of the Buddha. ^17-34

The great sound of your Dharma teachings spread throughout the ten directions.
Like a glorious wish-fulfilling jewel, you fulfilled the hopes and wishes of living beings.
You radiated the light of your blessings upon all those endowed with faith.
I supplicate you, whose enlightened activities were excellent. ^17-35

The confusion of ignorance was purified within the expanse of the true nature of reality.
Through the power of aspiration prayers, the qualities of nirvana fully blossomed.
You were the lineage-holding spiritual heir of all the lamas of the three times.
I supplicate you, whose supreme name was renowned everywhere. ^17-36

Your supreme name possessed four qualities, representing the four kinds of enlightened activities.
You dwelt in the supreme pure Buddha-field, free from all defilements.
You declared that your enlightened activities had been completed in the realm of Mirror-like Arrangement.
I supplicate you, who knew the past, present, and future. ^17-37

The wish-fulfilling jewel beautifully adorns the crown of the lord of the waters.
A city of bliss stands upon the summit of Mount Meru.
Through the glory of merit, I have witnessed the excellent qualities of my lama.
O kind and compassionate master, I can never forget your kindness. ^17-38

Just as a bee gathers the sweet essence from a garden of lotus flowers,
And just as a swan separates milk from the waters of the ocean,
Through my faith, I have received the spiritual accomplishments and blessings of my lama.
O kind and compassionate master, I can never forget your kindness. ^17-39

Although my lama has departed to the pure realm free from defilements,
Your compassionate vision knows neither distance nor nearness.
Please quickly catch me with the iron hook of bodhicitta.
Please draw me toward you with the lasso of the wisdom of emptiness. ^17-40

Please rescue me from the deep and dangerous ravine of self-grasping.
Please wash away the stains of faults, downfalls, and defilements.
Please release me from the iron chains of the eight worldly concerns and samsara.
Please remove the chronic disease of attachment to one-sided views and disturbing emotions. ^17-41

Please guide me onto the path of liberation through pure ethical discipline.
Please show me the ultimate view of the profound path.
Please cause the tender sprout of bodhicitta, the seed of Buddhahood, to grow within me.
Please enable me to attain the final result of omniscient Buddhahood. ^17-42

<small>This was composed by Skuzhang Lha Sonam Pal.</small> ^17-43
