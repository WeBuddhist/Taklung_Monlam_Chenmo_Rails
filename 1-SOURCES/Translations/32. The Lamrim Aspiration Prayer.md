# The Lamrim Aspiration Prayer ^0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-0]]

## The Lamrim Aspiration Prayer ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-1]]

Precious and incomparable masters of the Kagyü lineage,
The sacred Dharma taught by the omniscient Victorious One,
The noble Saṅgha, the yidam deities, and the Dharma protectors—
Please bestow your grace so that these aspirations may be accomplished exactly as wished. ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-2]]

For as far as the limitless expanse of space extends,
May all sentient beings who have been my fathers and mothers,
Completely transcend the miserable states in which there is no opportunity to practice the Dharma,
And obtain the precious human body endowed with the freedoms and favorable conditions. ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-3]]

May I meet a spiritual teacher rich in the treasures of learning and reflection,
Having been taught, and while listening to the sacred Dharma spoken by him
And may I not become like an upturned or contaminated vessel,
But with faith and devotion, may I reflect upon and meditate on the teachings. ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-4]]

All conditioned things are impermanent and do not remain stable even for a moment.
Since there is no certainty or security in this fleeting life,
May I continually maintain the understanding that worldly things are ultimately of no essential need,
And practice the sacred Dharma with a one-pointed mind. ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-5]]

May I carefully restrain myself from harmful conduct,
Practice the discipline of gathering all virtuous qualities,
And excellently accomplish the welfare of all sentient beings;
May I abide in the ethical discipline of the Mahāyāna. ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-6]]

Aspirational bodhicitta is the wish to attain perfect Buddhahood.
Engaging bodhicitta is the path of actually accomplishing that very goal.
It consists in training in the six pāramitās,
May I abide in the supreme vows of the bodhisattvas. ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-7]]

May the entire maṇḍala of deities of the generation stage appear vividly and clearly,
And may the samādhi of the completion stage be completely pure.
Without ever departing from the vast array of samaya commitments,
May I bring the supreme path of the profound Secret Mantra to perfection. ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-8]]

Unstained by the defilement of conceptual thought that grasps at characteristics,
May I realize completely pure mind-itself—the luminous Mahāmudrā.
Having directly encountered its naturally present, primordial nature,
May I guide fortunate beings along such a path. ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-9]]

May the teachings of the glorious Taklungpa, the King of Dharma,
Flourish and spread, and having entered into those teachings,
May I perfectly traverse all the paths leading to liberation,
And become one who liberates the beings of the three realms. ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-10]]

Furthermore, through the power and strength of these pure aspirations,
May I bring to perfection the attainments of the five paths and ten bhūmis,
And, possessing the nature of the four kāyas and the five primordial wisdoms,
May I swiftly attain the state of Vajradhara. ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-11]]

<small>At the request of the fully ordained monk Kunga Chökyab, this was composed by the Śākya monk.</small> ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-12]]

Homage to the Precious Guru! ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-13]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As indivisible, inseparable, and unconditioned.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-14]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As nonconceptual, free from thought, and like space.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-15]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As never deluded, not becoming deluded, and completely free from delusion.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-16]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As never objectified, not to be objectified, and free from any object of fixation.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-17]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As never moving, not moving, and free from movement.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-18]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As never changing, not changing, and free from change.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-19]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As uncreated, not created, and free from anything made or fabricated.
May we sentient beings realize this with certainty.
May we sentient beings realize this with certainty. ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-20]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As free from the effort and activity of a knower and something known.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize this with certainty. ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-21]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As never arisen, not arising, and free from arising.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-22]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As never ceased, not ceasing, and free from cessation.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-23]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As never abiding, not abiding, and free from any abiding.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-24]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
Such as form, to be nondual, yet not a single, unitary thing.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-25]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As free from color and shape, their essential nature completely pure.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-26]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As neither truly existent things nor simply nonexistent.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-27]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As utterly pacified of all signs of conceptual elaboration.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-28]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As completely pure in terms of the cause, the path, and the result.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-29]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As equality itself, with nothing to reject and nothing to accept.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-30]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
Throughout the three times and three realms, without exception, as completely pure.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-31]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As nondual, not divided into two, and of a single taste.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-32]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena
As the wheel of primordial wisdom whose very nature is luminosity.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-33]]

Precious One, you know all phenomena,
And know how to bring what is unequal into equality and mature those to be trained.
All phenomena that you know to be just so—
May we sentient beings realize them with certainty. ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-34]]

<small>Recite these verses repeatedly, joining the beginning and conclusion as before.</small> ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-35]]

<small>This was composed by Taklung Thangpa, the Sugata of the Dharmakāya.</small> ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-36]]

May I never be separated from the incomparable lordly gurus, the master and his spiritual sons,
And may I carry out whatever instructions they give.
May the delusion of my own mind be purified,
And may I become capable of being a supreme guide for wandering beings. ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-37]]

Wisdom is the cause, and wisdom is the path.
The fruition of wisdom is Mañjuśrī.
Precious Lord, you have perfected wisdom.
May I never be separated from the Precious Lord. ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-38]]

Compassion is the cause, and compassion is the path.
The fruition of compassion is Avalokiteśvara.
Precious Lord, you have perfected compassion.
May I never be separated from the Precious Lord. ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-39]]

Enlightened activity is the cause, and enlightened activity is the path.
The fruition of enlightened activity is Vajradhara.
Precious Lord, you have perfected enlightened activity.
May I never be separated from the Precious Lord. ^1-39

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-40]]

In particular, in this northern region, Avalokiteśvara,
Appearing in the manner of one who observes pure conduct, is the supreme holder of the Victorious One’s teachings.
May the practice lineage of the great and glorious TaklungThangpa,
Like the sun illuminating the day, spread throughout the entire world. ^1-40

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-41]]

May the community greatly increase, remain harmonious and inseparable, and always be joyful.
May even the names of illness, harmful spirits, and obstacles never be heard.
May completely pure sources of gathering merit and resources descend like rain.
Grant your blessings that the teachings may be upheld, protected, and spread. ^1-41

![[1-SOURCES/Text/32. ལམ་རིམ་སྨོན་ལམ.md#^1-42]]

Through the blessings of the Kagyü lineage gurus,
Through the truth of the siddhis of the yidam deities,
And through the power and strength of the Dharma protectors and guardians,
May the teachings of the practice lineage flourish and spread! ^1-42
