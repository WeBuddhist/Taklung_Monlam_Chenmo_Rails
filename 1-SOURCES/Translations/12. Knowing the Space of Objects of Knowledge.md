# Knowing the Space of Objects of Knowledge ^0

## Knowing the Space of Objects of Knowledge ^12-0

Vast mandala of those who know the space of knowledge
Brilliant deeds bestowed on the realm of disciples,
Illuminating all the doctrines of the Victorious Ones,
I prostrate to the Precious Kagyu! ^12-1

Abandoning the eight unfavourable states, perfectly obtaining the freedoms and endowments,
attaining supreme accomplishment, master of those who practice virtue,
Regent of the Sage, crown ornament of the Land of Snows,
I prostrate to peerless Gampopa! ^12-2

Perfect Guru, precious protector of beings,
coming to Shanti Mountain for the benefit of disciples,
acting for the benefit of others through the Dharmas of Scripture and Realization,
I prostrate to You, Regent of the Teacher! ^12-3

From his birth in the female earth sheep year onward,
until coming to the Place of Miracles,
all of your limitless, completely wonderful history
I will briefly recount, please listen! ^12-4

Lotus Guru Pushpachandra,
witnessed by the Buddha, you became “Youthful Moonlight.”
becoming a Bhikshu-Physician at this snow mountain retreat,
I prostrate to You, who hold the Victor’s prophesy! ^12-5

Born in the region of Nyal consistent with your aspiration,
you married a Lady in keeping with the customs of the world,
You bestowed unlimited healing medicine for the benefit of others,
I prostrate to You, human embodiment of Bodhicitta! ^12-6

Then seeing that Samsaric actions have no essence,
You left home and were ordained to the Dharma,
guarding the Three Trainings like your own eyeballs,
I prostrate to You, Master of Bhikshus! ^12-7

Placing the dust of the Holy Teacher’s feet on your head,
You learned vast teachings on Vinaya, Mantra and so on
Developing innumerable samadhis, attaining the dreamlike Bhumis,
I prostrate to You, Precious Merit! ^12-8

When you heard the Guru’s name through pure aspiration Bodhicitta,
With hairs quivering, You experienced supreme increasing joy,
You experienced signs in dreams and gained perfect qualities,
Fortunate person, I prostrate to You! ^12-9

When you came before the Venerable Milarepa,
He said, “You, precious for all sentient beings,
I consider to be my heart son and lineage heir!”
I prostrate to You, Teacher from Central Tibet! ^12-10

He gave the oral instructions for the supremely profound, Holy Path of Means,
With no distraction, You diligently practiced and accomplished
And realization, the Guru’s kindness, was born in Your mind,
I prostrate to You, diligent one! ^12-11

You entered the supreme Path of Secret Mantra
You reached the limits of the transmission of the qualities of the Ten Signs,
and became qualified to work with channels, nadis and bindu,
I prostrate to You, Lord of Yogins! ^12-12

When meditating on the four-session yoga of the Two Stages,
You met the Seven Medicine Buddha Brothers in the Sambhogakaya Pureland,
And met the Lord of Sages and the other Thousand Buddhas,
I prostrate to You, Mount Meru, Lord of Humans! ^12-13

You proclaimed praises for the sake of the Venerable Master!
Realizing the Three Kayas, attaining the Two Siddhis,
You thus attained the state of Bhikshu Vajra Holder.
I prostrate to the Venerable One, famous throughout the world! ^12-14

Completely transmitting all empowerments and Dharma without exception,
abandoning evil companions and embracing solitude,
Accomplishing the benefit of others, saying “Protect your stock of accomplishments!”
I prostrate to You, Great Being! ^12-15

When staying in Olka, realizing that all Dharmas like dreams,
and unreal, manifesting the state of Simplicity,
You said “this is my last rebirth!”
I prostrate to You, Lord of the Ten Bhumis! ^12-16

When you arrived at the place of DaklaGampo,
Keeping in mind to accomplish according to the Guru’s instructions,
You spoke the Dakinis’ prophecy for the benefit of others,
I prostrate to the Lord of Dharma Gampopa! ^12-17

Synthesis of all manifold fortunate, karmically favored ones,
You taught the Guru’s oral instructions, holy and profound,
You ripened and liberated limitless gathered beings
I prostrate to the peerless supreme Guru! ^12-18

For a time, you showed the four manifestations of Nirmanakaya,
Consecrating and teaching the Dharma to others,
flying through space, and staying in sealed retreat,
I prostrate to You, with power over all appearances! ^12-19

When training in the mental practice of profound Samadhi,
overpowering the elements, expanding and transforming,
Your stainless body emanated as Lord of the World,
I prostrate to You, miraculous Supreme Being! ^12-20

When staying among innumerable learned and accomplished ones,
overpowering others’ perceptions through the courage of Dharma,
You said, “I am Lord of the Doctrine!”
I prostrate to You, lion among humans! ^12-21

When meditating on the Illusion-like Samadhi,
You manifested the Great Enlightenment of Inflated Space,
and You showed unimaginable miracles,
I prostrate to You, inseparable appearance and emptiness! ^12-22

All those with faith saw you as Chakrasamvara,
transforming consecration into Great Enlightenment,
The Jnanasattvasactually dissolved into the Supports,
I prostrate to You, who achieved supreme accomplishment! ^12-23

All people with faith, wherever they may be,
just simply by seeing, hearing, and remembering You,
abandoning suffering, attain the Path of Liberation!
I prostrate to You, King of Physicians! ^12-24

When practicing the Mudra of the Method of Joining Amrita,
the three of body, appearance, and mind rub together and merge into one,
Your illusory body instantly emanates uncountable forms,
I prostrate to You, Royal Supreme Guide! ^12-25

When the thought arose in your mind to pass away for the benefit of others
On the fifteenth day of the sixth month of the female water bird year,
Your great being departed to the Dharmadhatu.
I prostrate to You, who passed into Nirvana! ^12-26

Many rainbow lights with parasols, and victory banners, and
rainfalls of flowers, and the sound of music occurred.
All the earth shook, and all the birds cried out,
I prostrate to You, amazing miracle! ^12-27

Innumerable people endowed with faith gathered,
presenting exquisite offerings of gods and humans,
You bestowed heart and tongue relics on those with faith,
I prostrate to You of altruistic deeds! ^12-28

Luminous Dharmakaya, naturally without elaboration,
Sambhogakaya, the natural appearance of Great Bliss,
Nirmanakaya ripening and liberating all beings,
To the Supreme Guide, the Guru with Your lineage, ^12-29

I offer the Cloud of Offerings of Samantabhadra, equal to space!
I individually admit all non-virtues of body, speech and mind!
I rejoice in all the virtue of myself and others!
I pray you to stay forever, turning the wheel of Dharma! ^12-30

Protector, when You were showing Enlightenment,
Even though you gazed at all beings with the eyes of compassion,
may I and all disciples not let it diminish,
Please satisfy us with the amrita of the Supreme Vehicle! ^12-31

The Guru’s feet remaining firm throughout all existence,
By making offerings to accomplish profit and honor,
and always remaining inseparable from the Teacher’s perception,
May I never be clothed by faulty thinking, ^12-32

And acting in the same manner as the deeds of the Guru,
Making supreme offerings with wonderful devotion,
and in all actions, not thinking of the faults of others,
May I continually accomplish beneficial thoughts! ^12-33

By my realizing enlightenment like Yourself,
For all sentient beings who equal space that become disciples,
Making a festival of Dharma with mantra and confidence,
May the sound of the Mahayana Dharma ring out! ^12-34

By prostrating and offering, confessing, and
Rejoicing after others, and exhorting supplication,
Whatever small amount of virtue I have accumulated,
not abiding in the extremes of existence, nonexistence, and so forth,
personification of the Four Kayas and Five Jnanas,
I dedicate all to unsurpassable complete Great Enlightenment! ^12-35

<small>So, this prayer, based on the biography of the complete liberation of the Lord of Dharma, Glorious Gampopa, along the with Seven Branches, was written at the Great Seat of Glorious Phagmo Drupa by the Venerable Phagmo Drupa himself.</small> ^12-36

Possessing the prophesy of the Tathagata,
You benefitted beings in these degenerate times.
You mastered the Mahamudra
I prostrate to Youthful Moonlight! ^12-37

Glorious Phagmo Drupa, in a forest retreat,
Ripening many fortunate disciples,
You showed various inconceivable miracles.
I pray to the precious protector of beings! ^12-38

<small>This was written by Ngawang Namgyal.</small> ^12-39
