# The Third Event of the Day (Sojong Vow) ^0

## The Third Event of the Day (Sojong Vow) ^1-0

The Guide, the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror, Tathagata, Arhat, perfectly complete Buddha, endowed with perfect knowledge and perfect conduct, ^1-1

Sugata, Knower of the World, ^1-2

piloting the ship that tames beings, ^1-3

unsurpassable guide of all gods and humans, ^1-4

I prostrate to the Buddha, the Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror, Glorious Victor Shakyamuni ^1-5

I make offerings, I go for refuge! ^1-6

### Praise and Prostration ^1-1-0

When the supreme one among humans was born,
He took seven steps on this great ground,
and said “I am supreme in this world.”
I prostrated to you, wise at that time! ^1-7

Your completely pure, mighty, supremely excellent body,
is like an ocean of wisdom, like a heap of gold,
whose fame radiates throughout the Three Worlds,
I prostrate to you, supremely accomplished Protector! ^1-8

Face like a stainless moon, endowed with the supreme signs,
I prostrate to you, who shines like gold!
Immaculate one, with no peer in the three realms of existence,
I prostrate to you, an incomparable omniscient one! ^1-9

Protector, endowed with great compassion,
who guides by omniscience,
Oceanic field of merit and qualities,
I prostrate to you, Tathagata! ^1-10

Free from all desires through purity,
with virtue that liberates from the bad states,
single source of supreme ultimate meaning,
I prostrate to the Dharma of Peace! ^1-11

Liberated, then showing the path to liberation,
training by abiding in supreme purity,
possessing all the qualities of the pure realms,
I also prostrate to the Sangha! ^1-12

### Sutra ^1-2-0

Do not commit a single degrading action!
Accomplish virtue perfectly!
Completely tame your own mind!
This is the doctrine of the Buddha. ^1-13

Like stars, cataracts, butter lamp flames,
illusions, dew, water bubbles, and-
dreams, lightning, and clouds,
View all composite dharmas in thar way. ^1-14

### Dedication ^1-3-0

By this merit, may I become omniscient,
and having attained it, eliminating all the enemies of faults,
may all beings be liberated from the stormy waves of birth, old age, sickness and death
of the ocean of existence. ^1-15
