# Praise of the Previous Lives of TaklungThangpa Rinpoche ^0

## Praise of the Previous Lives of TaklungThangpa Rinpoche ^1-0

Although your enlightened mind is completely pure and inseparable from the Dharmadhatu,
Through the various miraculous manifestations of great primordial wisdom,
You bring to spiritual maturity sentient beings as limitless as space.
I supplicate the glorious TaklungThangpa Rinpoche. ^1-1

In the eastern pure Buddha-field, free from all defilements,
To countless assemblies of bodhisattvas,
You continuously turn the wheel of the Mahayana Dharma.
I praise you, the victorious protector Akshobhya Buddha. ^1-2

Gathering together the compassion of all the bodhisattvas,
You manifested as a spiritual guide in this Saha world.
Your power, deeds, and enlightened activities are beyond imagination.
I praise you, the protector Avalokiteshvara. ^1-3

From the sacred treasury of Dharma in the land of Uddiyana,
You preserved the secret teachings of the Teacher’s enlightened body, speech, and mind.
You compiled the teachings and became a wheel- turning ruler among holders of knowledge-mantras.
I bow to Indrabhuti, the embodiment of primordial wisdom. ^1-4

Among the ocean of shravaka disciples who attended upon the Buddha,
You were supreme in retaining the teachings you had heard.
You compiled the vast collections of countless sutras.
I bow to the noble Ananda, renowned for his extensive learning. ^1-5

You were the supreme regent of the unequalled Teacher’s doctrine.
You served the great elders with devotion.
With the three kinds of nectar, you relieved the burning suffering caused by the three obscurations.
I bow to the noble lay devotee Dharmatala. ^1-6

In this snowy land of Tibet, you established the ten virtuous actions,
Making their royal laws like a golden yoke of discipline.
You opened the way for the sacred Buddhist teachings to flourish.
I pay homage to you, who appeared as King SongtsenGampo. ^1-7

Through the profound view and the vast conduct,
And through the blessed oral instructions of the lineage of practice,
You brightly illuminated the land of Tibet like a shining lamp.
I bow to DromtönGyalweJungne. ^1-8

Through serving your lama in the three ways that please him,
The auspicious circumstances for your enlightened activities naturally came together.
Your knowledge, realization, and compassion were beyond imagination.
I bow to the spiritual friend, the master Chayulpa. ^1-9

Outwardly, you maintained the training of individual liberation.
Inwardly, you practiced the generation and completion stages of the Vajrayana.
You became the life-supporting pillar of the teachings of their essential meaning.
I respectfully bow to the protector Mangalashri. ^1-10

From now until I attain unsurpassed enlightenment,
May I follow the liberating life and example of the incomparable Jetsun Taklung Thangpa.
May I train in accordance with his life of liberation,
And may I accomplish his three vajra secrets of enlightened body, speech, and mind. ^1-11

<small>This was written by the Buddhist monk Ngawang Tashi Paldrub.</small> ^1-12
