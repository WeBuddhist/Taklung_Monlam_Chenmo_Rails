# Aspiration of Pure Realm Free from Dust (Abhirati) ^0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-0]]

## Aspiration of Pure Realm Free from Dust (Abhirati) ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-1]]

The Outer Auspicious Aspiration for the Stainless Pure Realm OṃSvasti! In the outer world, in the eastern direction, is the Stainless Pure Realm.
There, the Victorious Protector Akshobhya
teaches the Dharma to a wondrous assembly of noble beings.
To Taklung Thangpa, his nirmanakaya emanation, I pay homage. ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-2]]

Within, upon the stainless ground of the Dharma wheel,
The pure essence of the channels, winds, and vital essences is the Victorious Akshobhya.
Teaching the Dharma of great bliss in the twenty-four sacred places,
to TaklungThangpa in sambhogakaya form, I pay homage. ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-3]]

Elsewhere, in the pure mandala of the northern direction,
stands Heruka, the Lord Master of the Mind’s Vajra.
the chief of the supporting celestial palaces and the supported deities of the five chakras.
I pay homage to TaklungThangpa as the dharmakāya. ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-4]]

Inseparably embodying the Three Bodies, the Master of all Buddha Families,
the Lord of Union (E-Vam) and Master of Vows—
and the embodiment of the compassion of all spiritual guides.
setup as a field of merit to purify all offerings. ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-5]]

Knowing your virtues, I pay homage with devoted faith,
touching the five points of my body—
numbered as the atoms of boundless oceans—to the earth.
I offer my body, possessions, ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-6]]

and all virtues throughout the three times,
transformed into clouds of offerings like those of the Bodhisattva Samantabhadra.
The offerings, the one who offers, and the recipient of the offerings cannot be found as inherently existing.
With these pure offerings, free from fixation on the three spheres, I fill every pure realm. ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-7]]

From the depths of my heart, filled with regret and remorse,
I confess all the harmful actions produced by the five poisons and committed through my body,
speech, and mind—those I have personally committed,
caused others to commit, or rejoiced in when committed by others. ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-8]]

Please purify all these wrong actions, together with
every related fault and downfall.
With complete joy, I rejoice in all virtue:
the virtue constantly present in the minds of beings, ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-9]]

According to the capacities, faculties, and inclinations of the ocean of beings to be guided,
please manifest the skilful means that lead them correctly to the genuine stages of realization.
Let the great wheel of the profound and vast Dharma break open-
and spread throughout the entire expanse of every realm and among all assemblies of disciples. ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-10]]

Although the Victorious Ones possess the unobstructed nature of space,
Before the bubble-like eyes of beings to be tamed, your form body
Displays its wondrous manifestations, while remaining unmoving and unchanging.
I pray that you remain and do not pass completely into nirvāṇa. ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-11]]

As represented by this accomplishment that brings the two accumulations into union,
Whatever accumulation of virtue has been gathered throughout the three times—
In accordance with the enlightened aspiration of all the Victorious Ones and their Bodhisattva heirs,
I dedicate every accumulation of virtue gathered throughout the three times—represented here by the united accumulation of merit and wisdom— ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-12]]

I dedicate it so that this excellent aspiration of mine may be fulfilled.
the excellent and ancient tradition of the supreme
TaklungKagyü lineage—
and its great Dharma teaching surrounded by the Four Jewels spread throughout the entire earth encircled by the oceans. ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-13]]

May the lives of the supreme learned and accomplished masters
who uphold the teachings remain firm and long.
May their many glorious and excellent qualities increase,
and may all their completely pure enlightened activities be accomplished exactly according to their wishes. ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-14]]

In all the realms of the world, may even the names of disease, conflict, famine,
and other causes of harm never be heard. May everyone obtain, exactly as wished,
all the desired conditions of happiness and joy.
May the happiness of a perfect age continually arise, bringing joy to everyone. ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-15]]

May I and all beings as limitless as space
obtain a fully qualified precious human life of freedom and favourable conditions, suitable as a support for the vows.
May we develop deep certainty concerning the frightening causes and results of saṃsāra,
and may we enter and completely practise the teachings of the Sugata, the Buddha. ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-16]]

With the intention of following the supreme sages,
and through the power of having the highest respect for the training,
may we never be stained by the impurities of faults and downfalls.
May we possess the complete perfection of higher ethical discipline. ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-17]]

Through the wisdom that correctly distinguishes all phenomena and through special insight-
fully supported by calm abiding,
may we overcome the enemy—the afflictions within our own mindstream—
and become a source of nourishment and support for the Buddha’s teaching and the ocean-like community of practitioners. ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-18]]

From the words of a fully qualified spiritual master of the Mahāyāna,
and through the ritual means by which their meaning is introduced,
may the wish-fulfilling jewels of the two kinds of bodhicitta-
fill the deep and vast ocean of our minds. ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-19]]

Through the power of this aspiration, reaching to the limits of space,
may we manifest whatever emanated forms are necessary beside each and every sentient being.
May we remove all the different forms of suffering that appear-
And strive to establish beings in lasting benefit and happiness. ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-20]]

Through the three higher trainings, the thirty-seven factors conducive to awakening,
and all the other vast practices of bodhisattvas and noble beings,
may we work solely for the welfare of beings without hesitation.
In this way, may we fulfil the enlightened intentions of all the Buddhas of the past, present, and future. ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-21]]

From the supreme vajra master who holds the awareness mantras,
may we receive the ripening empowerments that lead to the supreme accomplishment.
Through the realization that brings forth what must be abandoned and its remedies, and through the water of empowerment,
may the seeds of the four kāyas be planted perfectly within us. ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-22]]

May our body, speech, and mind be bound within the three sacred chakras.
May everything be naturally purified within the single expanse of dharmadhātu.
May we practise in accordance with the unsurpassed tantra—
the yoga uniting great bliss with supreme emptiness endowed with all aspects. ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-23]]

May all beings whose three poisons are powerful be introduced,
through the skilful means of the channels, winds, and vital essences
to their true nature as clarity, emptiness, and bliss.
Through the three vajras of enlightened body, speech, and mind, may awakening be bestowed upon them. ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-24]]

When the incomparable Dharma Lord had completed his enlightened activities,
He instructed his beloved disciples, saying,
“Offer your prayers to the Stainless Pure Realm.”
Through the truth of your words, O great sage, when we— ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-25]]

your disciples and subjects, together with every sentient being—
pray to the Stainless Pure Realm in the eastern direction, may the following aspirations be accomplished.
Immediately when the appearances of this present life cease,
may an inconceivably pure vision arise before us. ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-26]]

May fragrant flowers rain down,
and may assemblies of noble beings dwelling on the bodhisattva levels come forward to welcome us.
In the centre of a brilliantly coloured lotus,
may we obtain an excellent body adorned with the major and minor marks of an awakened being. ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-27]]

May we continually present unsurpassed-
offerings arranged in the manner of Samantabhadra’s vast deeds of liberation.
Through the power of the confidence arising from the most profound wisdom,
may we proclaim melodious praises of enlightened qualities. ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-28]]

The precious dharmakāya guide of this realm-
appears as the Victorious Protector Akṣobhya.
When he turns the wheel of the Dharma of the supreme vehicle,
may we be able to ask questions and answer them easily and confidently. ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-29]]

the sound of the sacred teachings that are virtuous in the beginning, virtuous in the middle, and virtuous in the end.
May we continually hear, from everything animate and inanimate,
May all the Buddhas and bodhisattvas proclaim with one voice-
the prophecy of our attainment of great awakening. ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-30]]

Rejoicing because of that prophecy,
like the king of swans spreading his wings.
may we rise into the sky to the height of seven tāla trees,
May we lovingly display many different kinds of miraculous manifestations. ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-31]]

May innumerable millions of beings look upon these manifestations with eyes of joy and devotion.
May that sight become the condition that opens for them the doors to hundreds of thousands of-
samādhis and enables them to receive the supreme prophecy of awakening.
and enables them to receive the supreme prophecy of awakening. ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-32]]

In brief, in that Stainless Pure Realm in the eastern direction,
may I also abide in supreme awakening, inseparable from the Protector Akṣobhya,
the supreme-minded bodhisattva, and the Stainless King.
may I also abide in supreme awakening, ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-33]]

For the present, may we be completely victorious over all unfavourable conditions.
May our life, freedom from death, glory, and prosperity increase.
May all our wishes and aspirations be fulfilled exactly as desired.
May the precious enlightened intention be brought fully to completion. ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-34]]

O Dharma Lord, source of an ocean of auspiciousness,
Through the power of extensively proclaiming your auspicious, wondrous, and excellent deeds,
may the supreme teaching of the auspicious TaklungKagyü lineage-
spread throughout the entire vast earth, filling it with auspiciousness. ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/29. རྡུལ་བྲལ་ཞིང་སྨོན་སྭ་སྟི་ཕྱི་རོལ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-35]]

<small>Thus, this prayer of aspiration for the Stainless Pure Realm in the eastern direction, entitled “A Melody Aspiring to the Vajra-Like, Imperturbable State,” was composed by Taklungpa Ngawang Namgyal at the great monastic seat. It is a Dharma teaching intended to be offered to all fortunate beings to be guided in the regions of Dokham and Central Tibet.</small> ^1-35
