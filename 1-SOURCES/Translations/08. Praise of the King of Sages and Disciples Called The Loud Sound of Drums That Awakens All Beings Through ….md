# Praise of the King of Sages and Disciples Called The Loud Sound of Drums That Awakens All Beings Through Great Equanimity ^0

## Praise of the King of Sages and Disciples Called The Loud Sound of Drums That Awakens All Beings Through Great Equanimity ^8-0

Ocean of Jewels’ generosity, the Two Accumulations
gathered and completely polished in the Dharmata,
by the Great Truth, inconceivable and completely pure,
placed as ornaments of the pure realms of the ten directions, all ^8-1

excellence subsumed into one, then descended here!
Peerless Lord of Dharma, Great Sage,
with your retinue of the assembly of noble Arhats,
arriving through the power of your heart vow, please abide here! ^8-2

Through the power of your loving aspirations, great, vast, and profound,
Gloriously arriving at the pinnacle of all the Victorious Ones,
Lord who turns the wheel of the Dharma of the Three Yanas
I bow down to the Entity of all Perfect Buddhas! ^8-3

Shiva on Mt Kailash, form of virtuous merit,
source of an ocean of the supports for benefit and happiness,
drum that dispels longing, holder of incense with pervading perfume,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand three hundred! ^8-4

Radiant glittering crystal cave of the Dharma of Accomplishment,
unconquered by the objects of evil Maras,
They have been completely assimilated and bound.
I bow down to Arhat with one hundred disciples! ^8-5

In the dense forest of trees with aromatic leaves
The supreme noble Arhat Vanavasin, who has exhausted all contamination,
His peaceful scorpion-finger mudra a wish fulfilling tree of jewels,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand four hundred! ^8-6

In the precious, dreamlike realm of Zang Ling,
while taming disciples as a loving friend,
holding beautiful golden earrings,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-7

Indra’s presence, naturally arrived at the lion throne
The great Arhat Vajriputra
Chamaradhara, who gives a cooling threatening mudra,
I bow down to the Arhat with a thousand retinue! ^8-8

By the gently flowing waters of the Yamuna River,
completely liberated from existence, perfectly endowed,
teaching the Dharma and remaining in equanimity, overcoming evil by a mere sight,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-9

In Kashmir, in the grove of the perfect Gesar,
Kanakavatsa, object of worship for the lineage of Nagas,
binding them with the precious lasso of compassionate words and meaning,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-10

With an excellent tall form on the island of Godaniya,
Supreme Kanaka, with most excellent wisdom from mindfulness,
sealed by resting evenly in samadhi,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-11

Quickly accomplishing your aspiration in the northern continent, Uttarakuru,
in the centre, the Lord of Dharma Speech, Bakula
patiently bestowing many different jewels, holding a mongoose,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-12

Meeting and expanding bliss in the island of perfumed cherries,
Rahula, with supreme reverence for the teachings of the Victors and Sons,
wearing the helmet of countless precious actions,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-13

At the royal palace of the renowned Vulture Peak Mountain,
Ksudrapanthaka, who supremely and completely pacified the afflictions,
in great stable awareness, holding the mudra of equanimity,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-14

Living five hundred lifetimes on the especially exalted island,
Pindola Bharadvaja, free of all evil,
reading a volume of the Buddha’s Word, holding a begging bowl,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand esteemed ones! ^8-15

In the Realm of the Three, on Indra’s crown ornament,
the Honored One, mantrinPanthaka,
reading a volume of the Pitaka of the Supreme Vehicle,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-16

Teaching about the breadth of all glittering manifestation,
Protecting from the abyss of samsara and nirvana, Nagasena,
holding a cooling, excellent vase of treasure,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-17

Expounding Vihula on the beautiful ornament of Mt Meru
the noble Shravaka Gopaka,
understanding the book of the Buddha’s teachings concerning time
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-18

By the banks of the Sindh River, on the peak of the snow mountains,
with the unchanging wealth of intelligence and liberation,
holding a Dharmakaya Maha Bodhi Stupa,
I bow down to the Arhat with one thousand two hundred! ^8-19

Spoken words that pacify and free from the darkness of conditioned existence,
Clouds of afflictions are scattered by the antidote of the wind
thousand lights of understanding exhaustion and non-arising,
I bow down to the Sun of happiness and benefit and his retinue! ^8-20

Older brother, heart son of all Sages without exception,
Lord Avalokiteshvara, who holds the seal of Amitabha,
In the manner of an Upasaka, holding a tail-fan and anointing vase,
I bow down to the great attendant of all Noble Ones! ^8-21

With joy in offering with reverent faculties,
in the presence of the Victor along with his disciples,
because limited offerings spread on a table were not possible,
I bow down to the Patron, the Great Ha Shang! ^8-22

In the east, Dhritarashtra guards by strength,
in the south, Virudhaka, born of the noble Mahayana family,
In the west, Virupaksha, wrathful at the enemies of the Dharma,
In the north, Vaishravana, who bestows everything desired, ^8-23

In that way, the mandala of purity alone
the support of those treasures of compassion, realization and omniscience,
by mindful awareness, stable motivation is unmistaken
with the clarity of understanding, I bow down! ^8-24

I make actually existing and mentally emanated offerings!
I lay aside all hindrances to the higher realms and liberation!
I rejoice in the great waves of vast Buddha Activity!
I pray that you guard the holy Dharma with no hesitation! ^8-25

Please benefit living beings today and for a long time!
By the virtue of the Dharma of Scripture and Realization,
So that disciples’ minds may grow and flourish forever,
Grant your blessings that all will be accomplished as we wish! ^8-26

<small>So, at the great seat of the Glorious Taklung, the Sangha were ordained as full Bhikshus, which they perfectly accepted over a half-month’s time. When taking the mending-purification vows, after reciting the
Pratimoksha Sutra, they requested this prayer, and the Bhikshu Ngawang Namgyal composed this with delight in the immaculate Upper Retreat. Virtue!</small> ^8-27
