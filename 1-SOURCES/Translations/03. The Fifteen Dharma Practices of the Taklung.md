# The Fifteen Dharma Practices of the Taklung ^0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-0]]

## The Fifteen Dharma Practices of the Taklung ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-1-0]]

### The King of Dharma ^1-1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-1]]

Dharma King, protector of all beings as limitless as space,
who has attained the state of unified non-learning,
manifesting the illusory net with great love,
I pray to Glorious TaklungThangpa! ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-2]]

Guiding all beings to liberation by showing his stainless temporary face,
showing the temple of his supreme form of the completely pure three secrets,
Glorious Guidance on his throne of unchanging rainbow light
I prostrate to the pure appearance of the Three Kayas of Great Bliss! ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-3]]

Accomplished Transcendent Conqueror in the midst of the stainless Sangha,
innate inseparability of expanse and original awareness,
who looks on all beings with eyes of great compassion,
I prostrate to Varahi’s self appearing Four Kayas! ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-4]]

From the expanse of nonconceptual compassion, with skilled methods,
although abiding in the single taste of unelaborate great bliss,
manifesting the levels of the Command Lineage to guide through existence and peace,
I prostrate to Indrabhuti and his retinue! ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-5]]

Solitary hero who manifests the union of bliss and emptiness,
natural wisdom, Vajra Yogini,
the Guide Kurukulle who accomplishes all goals,
I prostrate to the Six Excellent Precious Dharma Guards! ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-6]]

By praising the Six Deities, the cause of the Seven Founders of the Kagyu,
may all beings, through stainless virtue,
like finding a precious jewel, inseparable from perfection,
unite the Three Qualities in one activity! ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-7]]

<small>This was spoken by the Dharmakaya Sangye Yarjon.</small> ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-8]]

Dharmakaya of the Sugatas of the three times,
I pray to you, look with the eyes of compassion!
With no obstacles to Dharma from dependent phenomena,
I pray for the blessing of natural independent activity of the holy Dharma. ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-9]]

In a solitary place free from danger and the three distractions,
completely abandoning all worldly entanglements without exception,
accomplishing the essence of the holy Dharma in one lifetime,
bless me quickly! Look with eyes of compassion! ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-10]]

Perverted guides of the body, speech and mind, and
all obstacles from an undevoted mind of the three obscurations,
By the unlimited power of the Four Karmas,
I pray for the blessing that they all be pacified and purified without exception! ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-11]]

All memories, thoughts, karma, and everything I have done,
without being clothed by the stain of non-Dharma,
to completely purify and naturally transform them into Dharma,
I pray for the blessing of your precious power. ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-12]]

My own mind is primordially unborn and completely pure.
Without fearing temporary conditions, attaining realization,
resting balanced in the state of the luminous Dharmakaya,
I pray for the blessing of completely pure causes and results. ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-13]]

accomplishing the pure Four Karmas effortlessly and naturally,
Introducing that to whomever is to be taught,
unceasingly fulfilling the hopes of all disciples,
I pray you bestow blessings of freedom from all doubt! ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-14]]

<small>The above was spoken by Lord Sangye Yarjon.</small> ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-15]]

Completely exhausting all faults,
transforming them into the treasure of all good qualities,
and completing all final results,
I prostrate to all Gurus! ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-16]]

Having departed to the state of Dharmata,
though you never move from the state of Dharmakaya,
all of us, disciples, attendants, the six kinds of beings,
without forgetting our former commitments, ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-17]]

gathered in clouds by your great compassion,
experiencing bliss without any attachment,
the reality of luminous clarity with no attachment,
I pray that you send a great rain of uninterrupted, ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-18]]

supreme, stable awareness!
Though you never move from the state of non-arising,
may all the thoughts, happiness and suffering of myself
and all following sentient beings, ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-19]]

all be completely pacified in the river of equanimity,
compassion and love,
and be experienced as Great Bliss.
Though you abide within the state of the experience of non-appearance, ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-20]]

we pray that, by all of our supplications,
may whatever any of us wish for,
all our aims, be meaningfully accomplished.
Noble one, even though you have realized ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-21]]

that characteristics of things are destroyed,
through clearly knowing the three times,
and may all those who are engaged in accomplishment,
be held closely in your heart, we pray. ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-22]]

Completely pacifying and exhausting all elaborations,
may we, disciples who follow your command,
so far as we follow after you,
have all our faults purified and qualities completed! ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-23]]

Although understanding that happiness and suffering are the same,
by overpowering the suffering of aches and pains,
and establishing beings in happiness,
I pray you may pacify all sorrow! ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-24]]

throughout the three times without exception,
although the characteristics of distractions are pacified,
may all those who practice following your example,
when looking undistractedly ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-25]]

at the essential nature of mind,
attain independence through the power of investigating
in a forest solitude!
like a mother thinking of her children, ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-26]]

through your great compassion,
may I and all beings without exception,
be permanently liberated from illness and suffering,
and be blessed to attain omniscience! ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-27]]

Through the blessings of the Precious Lord,
may I and all limitless sentient beings
be filled to overflowing
with the original awareness of the Tathagatas, ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-28]]

and be ripened to consummation, we pray!
So that we all might accomplish the rare
and supreme yidams and dharmapalas,
completely pacify all obstructors, ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-29]]

and attain supreme realization itself,
we pray that you bestow vast blessings!
May the flowing river of love not be blocked,
and the roots of compassion be kept watered, ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-30]]

the green tree of Bodhicitta spread widely
and the flowers of experience and realization bloom,
so that the fruit of the two benefits may ripen!
Rare and supreme Gurus and Yidams, ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-31]]

Dharmapalas, Dakinis of the virtuous side,
when we all become distracted,
when in isolated retreat, helplessly drawn
by desire and clinging to outward appearances, ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-32]]

by understanding they are all like dreams,
please transform all inner grasping,
attachment, and desire into bliss!
All hateful thoughts that arise ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-33]]

are transformed into compassion.
All ignorance, drowsiness, and sinking
transform into the wisdom of unmanifest luminosity,
like snow falling on the surface of a lake.
Please bless me to remain within that state. ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-34]]

<small>Spoken by the Tathagata Pamo Drupa.</small> ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-35]]

Although completely pure like the limitless, empty sky,
Knowing each detail of Samsara and Nirvana exactly as they are;
You who manifest all kinds of activity for everyone,
Lord of Dharma, endowed with compassion and skill in means, ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-36]]

to the precious Father and Sons of the Three Times and Timelessness,
with devotion of body, speech and mind, I prostrate at your lotus feet!
Completely clearing the darkness of my own and others’ deluded minds,
may we completely realize the rootless mind in itself! ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-37]]

Dear Mother of great nonconceptual compassion,
to myself and all beings, all of your children,
to the supreme place of perfect stainless great bliss,
Please guide us with every kind of skillful means! ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-38]]

By requesting you to fulfill your ancient commitments
Dharma Lord, Tathagata of the Three Times,
Please fulfill all of mine and others’ devoted wishes, and
Grant that supreme realization will quickly arise! ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-39]]

The Dharma Lord’s commands were taught by the Great Sage.
Compassion and Great Bliss that never changes!
Please think of all of us, and grant your blessing, so that this delusion
swiftly becomes like that! ^1-39

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-40]]

All of my own and all other limitless beings’
accumulated karma and sudden conditions
Just like a mirror free of any rust
Please completely purify everything without exception! ^1-40

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-41]]

All the clouds of delusion, malice and conflict,
Cleared away by the strong wind of Original Awareness,
just like the vast mandala of the sun in the sky,
Please help us to quickly accomplish virtuous thoughts! ^1-41

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-42]]

By the virtue of respectfully making this prayer,
My I and all others attain the Dharmakaya in this life,
May the assembly of the Sangha always be harmonious, and
may the perfect Sangha accomplish vast benefit! ^1-42

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-43]]

<small>This also was spoken by Sangye Yarjon.</small> ^1-43

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-44]]

Hero who vanquished more than four foreign enemies,
Ishvara and the others, endowed with the sixfold omniscience and love,
displaying supreme qualities, adorned with all the marks and signs,
I prostrate to the Fathers and Sons who show the Three Criteria as one! ^1-44

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-45]]

Unmoving like the supremely solid Mount Sumeru,
Universal Emperor, without any equal,
effortlessly benefitting others, leading all to liberation,
Father and Son Sugatas of the Three Times, I prostrate to you! ^1-45

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-46]]

Three Lords, unconceived and inconceivable,
with the quality of limitless activity free from intention shaking the Three Worlds and establishing them in the
Supreme Realm
Guiding Dharma Lord Father and Sons, I prostrate to you! ^1-46

By the essence of the Sugatas of the Three Times, the Dharma Lord’s power,
Completely pacifying all outer and inner obscurations, harm, and hostility,
Endowed with mutually loving minds, attaining great bliss,

By this and all that I and others have accumulated in the Three Times,
In the eastern direction, the blissful immaculate Dharmata,

At the feet of Father and Son, Compassion itself, ^1-48

Myself and all others attaining unsurpassable supreme Enlightenment,
By the effortless Activity of the Single Character,
May the gathered disciples, as many as there may be,
be led to the state of the unsurpassed Dharma Lord! ^1-49

Avalokitesvara from this special Northern Lineage,
Supreme holder of the Victor’s Doctrine of the rules of pure conduct,

May the gathering increase greatly in unshakeable joy
Without even hearing the names of illness, evil, or obstacles,
May completely pure good fortune fall like rain
And bless that the Doctrine’s Guardians and Protectors increase! ^1-52

By the blessing of the Kagyu Gurus, and
the truth of the Yidams’ accomplishments, and
by the power of the Dharma Protectors and Guards,
may the doctrine of the Accomplishment Lineage grow and flourish! ^1-53

Venerable Ngawang Tashi Paldrup,
Lord of Dharma DrakpaLekdrup

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-63]]

One who manifests the most sacred Bodhicitta
as an uninterrupted flow of dramatic display,
precious Root Guru,

By this aspiration prayer of universal excellent activity,
appearing right now as the supreme deity,
glorious blood-drinking Heruka,
I pray to the assembly of Yidams! ^1-67

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-68]]

Transformed from grass and wood,
mother of all the Victors of the Three Times,
all samaya holders who work to protect,

You who made a commitment in the presence of the Buddha,
by the dragon’s roar of Hung and Phat,
destroyer of great heaps of obstructing Maras,
I prostrate to all the Dharmapalas! ^1-69

To accomplish Buddhahood in one lifetime,
with a means of living compatible with Dharma,
you provide a treasure of all accumulations without exception,
I prostrate to all the Wealth Deities! ^1-70

I pray to the Guru, the precious Dharmakaya!
I pray to the compassionate Root Guru!
I pray to the lineage of accomplished Gurus!
I pray to the precious Gurus of the Command Lineage! ^1-71

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-72]]

I pray to the Guru, the precious Buddha of the three times! ^1-72

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-73]]

I pray to all the Gurus, Yidams, Dakinis, Dharmapalas and the assembly of Guardians! ^1-73

I pray for blessings so that the minds of myself and all sentient beings may follow the Dharma! ^1-74

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-75]]

I pray for blessings so that our Dharma may follow the Dharma!
I pray for blessings so that our Dharma may follow the Path!
I pray for blessings so that the Path may clarify delusion!
I pray for blessings so that delusion may shine forth as Original Awareness! ^1-75

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-76]]

I pray for blessings to remember impermanence and death!
I pray for blessings to be aware of the faults of Samsara!
I pray for blessings for the diligence to attain enlightenment in one lifetime!
I pray for blessings so that Samsara and Nirvana may be liberated in its own ground! ^1-76

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-77]]

I pray for blessings for the attainment of the supreme real accomplishment of Mahamudra! ^1-77

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-78]]

You embody all the completely pure omniscience and love of all the Victors combined,
holder of the victory banner of resounding auspicious benevolence,
glorious protector of all Samsara and Nirvana in this fortunate age,
I prostrate to you, wonderful supreme Nirmanakaya! ^1-78

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-79]]

From former times, by the vast mountain of the north,
in the Kalapa palace in the land of Shambhala,
in the beautiful grove of Malaya,
I prostrate to the supreme Rigden King! ^1-79

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-80]]

Then, in this valley surrounded by snow mountains,
bearing the name of Marton Rinchen Shakya,
authentic disciple of the powerful Shawaripa
I prostrate to you, who benefits all beings as they desire! ^1-80

Protector of Thatness, who now appears in this pure realm,
supreme siddha of the famous Wolf Valley,
one hundred year old Vidyadhara, ShonnuLodro
I prostrate to you, learned and accomplished universal crown ornament! ^1-81

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-82]]

Just like the lake born Padma,
naked of any stains to quality or realization,
actual unchanging vajra of supreme body, speech and mind,
I prostrate to the Guru, our protector until the end of existence! ^1-82

In that way, the garland of all the supreme births,
understanding everything, then arising to benefit others,
powerful Vajradhara, lord of the Four Kayas,
I prostrate to the Guru, the embodiment of all the Victors! ^1-83

By my praying and prostrating to you with reverence,

by the protector of the Three Realms, Namgyal Tashi,

And for now, may whomever is endowed with the qualities of the higher realms
be completely victorious over all intervening obstacles,
and abiding within the view and conduct of noble joy,

<small>This was written by the Lord’s own experienced disciple, Ngawang Namgyal, then clarified by the noble Jetsun Drolchok based on his example.</small> ^1-86

Glorious Victors of the Three Times, without exception,

Unchanging Vajra Body, Vairocana,
Brahma’s voice of Vajra Speech, Amitabha,
Mind Vajra of Great Bliss, Victor Akshobhya,

beginning with this pure, vast, aspiration prayer,

To the glorious Guru, the embodiment of all Victors,

may all beings, without exception, who fill all of space,
Accomplish the Guru Vajradhara! ^1-91

From this life onward, as long as enlightenment has not been attained,
From the Supreme Master who shows the essence of the holy Dharma,
the Glorious Guru himself, never being separate,
may I become a vessel for all the vast, profound Dharma! ^1-92

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-93]]

Through stable reverence, devotion, trust, and certainty,

by showing various forms to tame whomever is to be trained,
May all beings who pervade space attain complete enlightenment! ^1-94

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-95]]

May the intentions of the glorious Guru be fulfilled,
and may happiness arise for all beings that equal space,
and myself and every other being purifying obscurations and gathering accumulations,
Grant the blessing of swiftly attaining Buddhahood! ^1-95

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-96]]

<small>This is Bhikshu Ngawang Namgyal’s supplication to his Guru, the great Vajra Master Namgyal Rinchen Pal Zangpo.</small> ^1-96

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-97]]

Very embodiment of the omniscience and love of all Victors,
Like a god, manifesting Nirmanakaya for the benefit of beings,
Glorious Taklungpa, Gelek Jungnay,
I pray to Venerable Ngawang Namgyal! ^1-97

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-98]]

Lord of the World, known as the Incomparable Tangpa,
By manifesting everything as Dharmakaya,
multitude of appearances as the Garland of Golden Mountains of the Accomplishment Lineage,
I pray to the supreme all pervading Lord! ^1-98

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-99]]

As the Buddhas of uncountable previous ages
depending on immeasurable Great Compassion,
manifested in limitless realms for whomever was to be tamed,
I pray to the one who causes the Buddha’s Teachings to increase! ^1-99

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-100]]

Particularly, in this home of the Supreme Accomplishment Lineage,
By knowingly, fully, accepting the world,
with a mind that is the cause for many virtuous qualities arising,
I pray that you give rise to wonders everywhere! ^1-100

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-101]]

In this fortunate aeon, rich with the wealth of faith and wisdom,
From Vajradhara to the Venerable Masters of the past,
All you Lords of the Kagyu yourselves,
I pray you will manifest your promise! ^1-101

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-102]]

Through completely fulfilling this inexhaustible vow,
to guide all disciples gradually through the Dharma,
I pray that the ordained sangha be free of any impurities and defects,
and that they be endowed with stainless ethical conduct! ^1-102

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-103]]

Giving rise to the amazing, supreme Mind of Awakening,
gathering disciples by actualizing the unified Six Perfections,
establishing them in the higher states and Great Enlightenment,
I pray at the feet of the Great Bodhisattva! ^1-103

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-104]]

Supreme essence of an ocean of Vidyadharas and Scriptures,
through the unimpaired Kagyu blessing of Transference
Attained the highest realization of the profound Path,
I pray to the Supreme Lord of Siddhas! ^1-104

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-105]]

You, the fearless hero, victorious in all directions,
By your effortless, unceasing Enlightened Activity,
establishing all beings on the Bhumi of Great Bliss,
I pray at the feet of the Supreme Guide! ^1-105

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-106]]

For myself and all disciple beings without exception,
pacifying all situational and ultimate obstacles,
And for all wealth and glory, perfect and consistent with Dharma,
Please grant the blessing of them increasing more and more! ^1-106

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-107]]

<small>So, this was offered with devotion by the Buddhist monk Namgyal Rinchen Pal Zangpo at the Great Residence, when he closely, perfectly beheld the mandala of the face of the Supreme Venerable Nirmanakaya himself.</small> ^1-107

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-108]]

Treasure of an ocean of everything perfect,
Filled with the Dharma of the Essence of the Supreme Victor
Holder of the special, noble Complete Liberation, Taklungpa,
I prostrate to Ngawang Tashi Paldrup! ^1-108

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-109]]

Garland of emanations of ultimate blessing and realization,
Supreme Nirmanakaya of the Perfect Buddha,
single Original Awareness appearing in successive forms,
I prostrate to the one with the power of limitless compassion! ^1-109

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-110]]

Great Vidyadhara Sangye Yeshe, and
The one called Drington Shakya, who completed the Three Trainings,
The one endowed with the meaning of seeing and hearing, Orgyen Gonpo, and so on,
I prostrate to NamrolPakye Tenpa! ^1-110

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-111]]

So, the nondual Kaya of Mahamudra
One who knows all subjects, with powerful certainty,
Protector of the nine births of the Victory Banner of the Accomplishment Lineage Dharma
I prostrate to Master Tsemar Gyurpa! ^1-111

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-112]]

Then, as when at the top of a pure, green, wish-fulfilling tree,
an exquisite flower of the signs and examples shines,
that gives limitless joy and fulfilment with its quality and aroma,
I prostrate to the one who bestows the blessing of all desired accomplishments! ^1-112

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-113]]

Inexhaustible ornament of the inconceivable Three Secrets,
Effortless, Innate, Door of Liberation,
Visibly Manifest Nature, as has always been,
I prostrate to Choden YirabTrokpa! ^1-113

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-114]]

The wild geese of all sciences, without exception
Arise suddenly from desiring composite things
Inexhaustible intelligence that joins with whatever experience
I prostrate to the one with the power of vast, learned speech! ^1-114

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-115]]

You gathered the Two Accumulations with great power of meditation,
The Lord of the Family, supremely, effortlessly, and in a short time,
Bestowed profound and vast empowerments, authorizations, and key instructions
I prostrate to NodzayChokyi Gyamtso! ^1-115

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-116]]

Deeply immersed in the words and meaning of sutras, tantras, and commentaries,
thinking closely of his former studies and understandings,
having attained a never failing memory, his speech had no equal.
I prostrate to the Great Self-Arisen Omniscient One! ^1-116

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-117]]

Immeasurable self-arising courage,
Having heard extensively, his purity of heart increases,
Unity well attained, unobstructedly increasing
I prostrate to Nyen NgakPawoi Doda! ^1-117

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-118]]

Embodiment of the Realization of Venerable Sangye Yarjon
Supremely shining mirror of all conduct,
Abiding as he is, able to withstand analysis like gold,
I prostrate to the one who is wealthy in completely pure ethical conduct! ^1-118

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-119]]

You, the heir to all the Sages and their Sons,
Marvellous Lord of Bodhicitta,
By acting in great waves of the Bodhisattva’s conduct,
I prostrate to the only supreme guide of beings! ^1-119

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-120]]

EH, emptiness supremely endowed with all wisdom,
WAM, the supreme skillful means of Unchanging Great Bliss,
Their union is the Lord of all Buddha’s Vows,
I prostrate to the Great Vajra Master! ^1-120

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-121]]

Knowing that all Dharmas in the apparent world are desireless and unobstructed,
Pervading, permanent, non-referential compassion, without measure or clinging,
You, protector with the power to free the bonds of Samsara and Nirvana,
By the continually abiding, indestructible, essential bindu, ^1-121

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-122]]

May myself and others on the path of devotion, all disciples,
effortlessly accomplish all of our virtuous aims,
and may the Glorious Taklungpa’s famous
Dharma be a beautiful ornament for the world! ^1-122

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-123]]

<small>The peerless precious Lord of Dharma Ngawang Tashi Paldrup made this praise at the feet of the supreme NgedonChokyi Nyima Namdrol. Called the “Loud Drum of the Cloud of Faith,” this praise was requested by the Vajra Disciple DesheDamtsik’s patron, the wealthy Ngawang ChojinGyutso, and written by the Bhikshu Ngawang Namgyal in the eastern subtle channel, the supreme pure land of Vajra Mirror. Virtue and Happiness!</small> ^1-123

### The Prayer called “Precious Body” ^1-3-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-124]]

I pray to the venerable precious Body!
By the blessings of the precious Body,
may this, my ordinary body of ripened karma
attain the stainless Rainbow Body, I pray! ^1-124

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-125]]

I pray to venerable precious Speech!
By the blessings of precious Speech,
may all the stains of my meaningless speech be purified,
and may I attain the sixty qualities of the Voice of Brahma! ^1-125

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-126]]

I pray to the venerable precious Mind!
By the blessings of precious Mind,
may my mind stream, caught in the net of habitual tendencies,
attain the Mind of the Five Original Awarenesses! ^1-126

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-127]]

by the results of my accumulating merit,
by supremely serving the Guru with body and speech,
may those who accomplish with faith and reverence,
effortlessly attain experiences! ^1-127

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-128]]

The Guru’s blessing is the supreme cause
of the emanations of the Buddhas of the three times,
The disciple’s devotion is the supreme cause
of cutting the root of the foundation of samsara. ^1-128

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-129]]

Stopping the rain of material things is the cause
of developing revulsion for the appearances of attachment.
Beings like abandoned children are the cause
that increases the enlightened activity of loving mind. ^1-129

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-130]]

Venerable, incomparable Guru, Father and Sons,
and myself, remaining inseparable by my accomplishing whatever you command,
may mind’s delusions be purified, and
may I become a supreme guide for beings! ^1-130

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-131]]

At the time when I arise as an illusory body,
may the appearances of the Bardo not arise,
may the suffering of death shine forth as bliss,
and may I rest within the mind of luminosity! ^1-131

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-132]]

May the holy Dharma of the Vihara vastly expand!
May all limitless sentient beings be happy and blissful!
May the three bad states of Samsara become exhausted and empty!
May the classes of Maras with evil karma be pacified and tamed! ^1-132

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-133]]

May all Bodhisattvas, wherever they abide,
accomplish all of these aspiration prayers! ^1-133

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-134]]

<small>Spoken by the Sugata Taklungpa.</small> ^1-134

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-135]]

The “Namo Ratna Guru Dharmakaya”: ^1-135

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-136]]

All Great Beings, whomever they may be, wonderful and amazing,
with completely pure, stainless, luminous compassion,
completely abandoning all the obscurations of dualistic clinging,
I prostrate to You, Stainless Dharmakaya! ^1-136

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-137]]

All apparent phenomena in Samsara and Nirvana,
the indivisible, spontaneously accomplished natural Body,
unmoving from the equanimity of supreme Enlightenment,
I prostrate to You, nondual Great Bliss! ^1-137

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-138]]

without moving from supreme natural equanimity,
by the enlightened activity of Your Body, Speech and Mind,
You establish all beings in supreme enlightenment!
I prostrate to You, transcendent enlightened activity! ^1-138

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-139]]

By the precious refuge of the inseparable Three Kayas,
Body, Speech and Mind of the marvellous Victors and their heirs,
protector of beings who clears away all darkness,
I bow down to the Lord Regent’s supreme attainment! ^1-139

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-140]]

The biography of Your noble birth is extremely profound,
though without the acts that transcend the conceptual mind,
just the apparent taming the assembly of disciples,
I prostrate to your inconceivable biography! ^1-140

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-141]]

Your stainless ethics,
completely pure,
I prostrate to You,
completely pure ethics! ^1-141

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-142]]

All beings, without exception, who equal space,
by clothing them in the armor of ripening,
You establish all beings in supreme Enlightenment!
I prostrate to You, endowed with incomparable love! ^1-142

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-143]]

In all the great places, Orgyen and so on,
By pleasing all the assemblies of Viras and Yogins, and
all the assembled Dakinis, queens of the expanse,
I prostrate to You, stainless co-emergence! ^1-143

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-144]]

Completely abandoning all worldly elaborations,
With the activity of Rishis, abiding in the solitude of mountain hermitages
Banner of renown, famous throughout the Three Worlds,
I prostrate to the one who accomplishes all that is seen, heard, and intended! ^1-144

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-145]]

You, Supreme Person with an ocean of qualities,
for those like me, whose darkness is difficult to describe,
by the power of devotion, by even a little teaching,
unequalled great bliss, Lords of Dharma, Father and Sons ^1-145

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-146]]

until the ocean of samsara is emptied,
without being separated from you, even for an instant,
May all beings be established in supreme Enlightenment! ^1-146

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-147]]

To the Dharmakaya, Taklung Tangpa,
from JiktenGonpo of the Drigung,
I praise you with reverence!
May the Doctrine pervade all the great Bhumis! ^1-147

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-148]]

<small>This was written by DrigungKyobpa, and the last shloka was added as a praise by Venerable Ngawang Namgyal, incorporating some of Thangpa’s extensive biography in 17 chapters, and spoken based on this work.</small> ^1-148

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-149]]

Homage to Guru Limitless Jewels! ^1-149

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-150]]

Unequalled Dharma Lord, endowed with meaning and supreme signs,
king of wish-fulfilling power, who fulfills all needs, desires, and hopes,
glorious protector of existence and peace, meaningful to see,
I pray to you, deceased supreme being! ^1-150

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-151]]

Endowed with the sixty qualities of Brahma’s precious speech,
disciple’s Lord Protector, who shows the unmistaken path,
resounding with the holy dharma of the wonderful Supreme Vehicle,
I pray to you, deceased supreme being! ^1-151

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-152]]

Precious Mind, stainless and completely pure,
protector of beings, holding the treasure of supreme compassion,
continually beneficial, thinking only of the welfare of others,
I pray to you, deceased supreme being! ^1-152

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-153]]

source fulfilling all needs and desires, Lord of Dharma who cares for the Nagas,
wish-granting jewel who fulfills all disciples’ hopes and desires,
beyond expression, with qualities difficult to measure,
I pray to you, deceased supreme being! ^1-153

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-154]]

Conqueror of the four classes of Maras, endowed with the six powers,
Great compassion, accomplished conquering King of Sages,
effortless activity gloriously shining forth in Existence and Peace,
I pray to you, deceased supreme being! ^1-154

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-155]]

Supreme being, endowed with Body, Speech, Mind, and Qualities,
Vajradhara, indivisible perfect activity,
realizing the inconceivable Three Secrets,
supremely joyful, please hold me in your heart! ^1-155

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-156]]

by the virtue of supplicating and prostrating to you,
in that stainless radiant eastern pure land,
at the feet of the Guides, the Lords of Dharma, father and sons,
may I be born in the miraculous lotus throne. ^1-156

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-157]]

<small>So, this praise of the Late Supreme Being, Ratnanatha, spoken by the Venerable Sangye Wonpo Dorje, was clarified slightly by Taklungpa Ngawang Namgyal and, in arranging it, none of my own work was added.</small> ^1-157

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-158]]

Homage to the Precious Guru Hevajra! ^1-158

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-159]]

Glorious, indestructible, powerful vajra body,
precious refuge, faultless and completely pure,
by the light rays of original awareness, meaningful to behold,
and clearing away all ignorance, I praise Lord Yarjon! ^1-159

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-160]]

Foundation of unobstructed gloriously arising qualities,
song of the great resounding emptiness of stainless supreme illumination,
Mother who has attained the result of the supreme speech of the Mahayana,
I praise you, Lord of Dharma who destroys the Four Maras! ^1-160

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-161]]

Mind realizing the omniscience of nonconceptual luminosity,
unchanging hero, treasury of all Victorious Ones,
expanse of Mahamudra, the nonduality of Samsara and Nirvana,
I praise you, completely enlightened Lord of Dharma! ^1-161

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-162]]

Vajradhara, accomplished conqueror, compassionate ruler,
who skillfully and lovingly guides all sentient beings,
Precious Guru, wish-fulfilling treasure,
I praise you, Lord of Dharma, the glory of Samsara and Nirvana! ^1-162

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-163]]

Accomplishing all wishes and aims just as desired,
expanse of the inseparable, nondual, self-liberated mind,
indefinite, unmeasurable Thatness
I bow at the feet of the Dharma Lord, Great Bliss of the Expanse of Awareness! ^1-163

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-164]]

By the blessings of reverence for You, precious one,
May I and all sentient beings who equal space,
Purifying all obscurations of body, speech and mind,
realize qualities equal to the Threefold Lord! ^1-164

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-165]]

<small>This, written by the essence of all Buddhas, Precious Guru Sangye Yarjon, who was the nephew of Sangye Won, his mind filled with supreme joy at meeting a yogin of Hevajra, Avadhutipa, a perfect Rishi, in the snake year, the monkey month, on the tenth day of the waxing moon, is completed.</small> ^1-165

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-166]]

All Tathagatas, illusory original awareness,
Magical fame, consistent with the Lord’s intention,
completely enjoying all the objects of thought,
I pray to you, glorious, famous, supreme emanation! ^1-166

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-167]]

By natural, effortless, purposeful action,
glorious, learned, venerable, excellent one who has left the top,
foundation of the perfect Buddha who has realized luminosity,
I pray to you, glorious, renowned supreme emanation! ^1-167

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-168]]

Instantly, all the environment and beings of Do Kham,
the actual immaculate teacher and students,
Amazing holy accomplishment, written by his hand,
I pray to you, glorious, renowned supreme emanation! ^1-168

![[1-SOURCES/Text/03. སྟག་ལུང་པའི་ཆོས་སྤྱོད་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཞུགས་སོ.md#^1-169]]

By the aspiration prayer of the conduct of Samantabhadra,
King of all the beings of destructible Samsara,
Quickly accomplishing, speaking evenly until the end,
grant your blessing that all falsehoods may be stirred out from the depths! ^1-169
