# This is the Supplication of the Kagyu called the Manika Crystal ^11-0

<small>In the presence of Jedrung Rinpoche at the upper retreat of Rachung, on the occasion of consecrating a painting of the lineage masters, I heard you teach on the heritage of the deity. When I supplicated, Won Heruka with all his attributes appeared in the thangka, and I offered the seven branches and prayed. He was adorned with the attributes of ordination, with vajra and kartari in his left hands and his right holding a ghanta, and kapala filled with amrita. Then I requested the amrita from the great powerful Hevajra. When the great powerful Hevajra gave it to me, the swirling amrita that I was able to drink had an inexhaustible, extremely sweet flavour, and as I sipped by stages, I thought that this dharma was useless. I said I was unable to finish it, and as I said that, he snapped his fingers and said drink one more mouthful! Arising from the mandala, the Great Powerful One gave it to me, and the one called Lilavajra prayed: “Please bestow all your collected teachings on us!” “In order to bless the disciples, a ganachakra vast feast offering is necessary,” he said. Offering the ganachakra, he spoke these words: “Enjoy this inexhaustible ganachakra as great original awareness!” Then, we said, “A La La! Please give us a prayer to the Kagyu Rinpoches!” Then he spoke this prayer called the “Supplication of the Manika Crystal.”</small> ^11-1

In the space-like Dharmata,
Taking up the form of the Great Rupakaya,
Sambhogakaya supremely adorned with the Signs and Examples,
Sixth Buddha of the Family of Signs, Vajradhara,
I pray to that Guru!
Venerable one, please grant your blessings! ^11-2

You took human birth in the Brahmin Caste,
holder of the six lineages of the special oral transmission,
overpowering many Tirthikas,
this precious doctrine of the Practice Lineage,
spreading like a blazing torch,
You are famous as Tilopa,
I pray to that Guru!
Venerable one, please grant your blessings! ^11-3

Scorning all suffering,
You performed twelve great austerities
and supremely pleased Tilopa.
You were filled with Holy Dharma like a vase,
You gained the siddhi of the Dakini’s Rainbow Body
You are famous as Jnana Siddhi!
I pray to that Guru!
Venerable one, please grant your blessings! ^11-4

Glorious Hevajra,
perfectly seeing all beings with compassion,
Emanating HUNG from your heart,
Famed as the Teacher from Mar, Dharma Mati,
You filled the realm of Tibet with Dharma!
I pray to that Guru!
Venerable one, please grant your blessings! ^11-5

Accepted by that Venerable One,
inserting prana-mind into the central channel,
realizing all of whatever arises to be Dharmata,
Omnisciently, clairvoyantly knowing the minds of others,
You are known as Vajra Victory Banner
I pray to the Great Father Repa!
Venerable one, please grant your blessings! ^11-6

Holder of the prophecy of the Great Sage of the Shakyas
Born near the region of Nyal,
You completed the study of healing and medicine,
and joined the two rivers of Kadampa and Mahamudra
holding both the lineages.
You are famous as the “World Renowned!”
I pray to that Guru!
Venerable one, please grant your blessings! ^11-7

Accepted by that incomparable one,
you were able to emanate one billion forms
in a single night
Bound by the commitment of divine powers and so on,
You are famous as Dorje Gyalpo (Phagmodrupa)!
I pray to that Guru!
Venerable one, please grant your blessings! ^11-8

Bhagavan Protector Akshobhya himself,
In order to establish many beings in happiness,
You guide myself and all other beings
Your famous signs are extremely difficult to describe
You are famous as Venerable Supreme Guide Tashi Pal!
I pray to that Father Guru!
Father, quickly hold with your compassion! ^11-9

The unmistaken precious lineage
of the glorious Taklung Kagyu
Held by the Venerable Holy Lord,
All those of the True Lineage, I supplicate you!
Venerable one, please grant your blessings! ^11-10

I pray that all the Gurus’ compassionate,
blessing may abide in my mind!
I pray to all the Yidams!
Send down ordinary and supreme accomplishments like rain! ^11-11

I pray to all the Dharmapalas!
Destroy all the masses of Tirthika Maras!
I pray to all the Wealth Deities!
Bestow the accomplishment of the jewel of all desirables! ^11-12

Beginning with this virtue,
May the precious Doctrine of the Buddha
and it’s essence, this Doctrine of the Practice Lineage,
continually pervade and expand
in all directions, times and countries!
May the doctrine holders’ bodies and lives remain stable, and
may all beings act in accord with Dharma! ^11-13

<small>The Yogin of Hevajra wrote this for the benefit of Gegpay Dorje. It is said that by hearing this supplication just one time, the blessing enters one’s mind and it is impossible to be separated from the Kagyu. So, then, fold your hands above your head and hold this in mind. After I prayed for this extremely extensive supplication, I felt that I didn’t need any other prayers. It is the blessing of inseparability. So, if you ask what my name is, I am called Shakya Trashi Kirti Ratna Sherab Pal Zangpo Dud de Choklay Namgyal. When I was staying at Rinpoche’s seat, I asked how the Seat could grow and flourish, and he said “you must care for this Seat and embrace it continually. This growing and flourishing may take almost as long as Lokeshvara’s commitment.” Then I asked if it would be appropriate to tell this to others, and he replied “very appropriate!” Then Kirti Ratna Su Siddhi also asked: “Please tell all the faithful ones!” “Teaching those without samaya is a great commitment. Explain the reasons!” “Resting the mind is the great essential point,” he said. I asked if establishing others in Dharma for fame and disciples isn’t a good thing to do? He said, “this is like the sound of ringing a hand bell with the strength of a white lion. Your fame is also like this. If your relationship with disciples is like Lokeshvara’s motivation of old, then it’s that way,” he said. Then I prostrated. I prayed, “may the Buddhadharma in general, and particularly the accomplishment lineage of the Taklung Kagyu Dharma grow and flourish! And especially, may the MarthangTaklung Dharma of Riwoche grow and flourish! May I be endowed with the good fortune to be able to establish in Dhamra all the regions of Do Kham.” Saying “may it become like this,” I snapped my fingers three times. Then afterwards, Lilavajra spoke three times: “What is your connection with Yarjon today?”Hearing these unpleasant words, I asked “what is the point of that?” “There is no point,” he replied. “Well then, I have a powerful reliquary with the the amrita of Krsnari and the authority of the Dharmapala of the Tent.” He gave the torma empowerment of the Great Powerful Mandala of Krsnari. For the secret initiation, he assigned HerukaTrinlay Dupa Tsal with two hands holding varja and ghanta. Bestowing the daily practice text and so on of HevajraKrsnari, he said “benefit beings!” Then I said, “I don’t think I’ve received Rinpoche’s supplication!” “What does this evil person say? I have one answer, that the Rosary of Kagyu is self arisen from marble.” Then bestowing the self-arisen Dharmapala of the Tent from the Black Rock Charnel Ground, and others, he said “this is to stabilize your faith.” Thus Je Choktrul Rinpoche, on the twenty-eighth day of the eighth month of his fifteenth year, a prophesy arose during a life accomplishment, excellently wrote this afterwards.</small> ^11-14
